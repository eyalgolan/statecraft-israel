# Military and combat (war of 1948)

## Player story

On 15 May 1948, with the Provisional government declared, the player takes command of the Haganah, which became the IDF at the end of May 1948 [S10]. The armies of Egypt, Iraq, Lebanon, Trans-Jordan and Syria enter [S10], and the question the whole run has been building toward arrives at once: hold the country. The player moves battalion-size units across the map they already know, trains fresh soldiers at the military camps they built in the struggle years, and spends the Arms their sourced arms deals brought in. Every front fight is decided by a strength table and one draw of fate, and the goal is never to destroy the enemy — it is to hold until the armistices are signed, one front at a time, until Syria signs last on 20 July 1949 [S2].

## Rules

- The location roster defines map priorities, including the Arab-held towns and regional hubs. It does not assign exact garrisons, make every location a capture objective, or introduce enemy reinforcements or supply rules. Starting and reference control is sourced; campaign ownership follows simulation state, with residents separate from combatants [S13].
- For the MVP the terrain types are the eight biomes, with the per-biome movement cost below (`Q-MOVE-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). The owner froze that catalogue for the MVP on 23 October 2026, with no new types and no terrain conversions [S16]; the location/editor update does not select new terrain types, cost values or modifiers [S1][S14].
- A unit is a formation of hundreds of fighters, never a single soldier (impact spec §2.2 [S8]; `Q-UNIT-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A finished recruit cohort becomes one unit at its city's settlement hex. Unit types on screen are Recruit (טירון), Veteran soldier (חייל ותיק), Artillery (ארטילריה) and Armoured car (שריונית) — names approved in English and Hebrew (`Q-UI-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S7]). A recruit promotes to veteran after a number of turns of service (labelled design value); combat does not count toward it (`Q-UNIT-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A unit's Strength counts its population units and drives movement allowance and combat strength (`Q-UNIT-1`); where a side's historical strength is sourced, the record enters the content as one value per CONTENT_POLICY §1 (`Q-UNIT-4`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]).
- Historical mode seeds the player's starting units from `content/map/units_1948.yaml`, each keyed to a sourced anchor (`Q-START-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). The Arab armies, the Arab Liberation Army and local Arab forces take part under their own names, with their 15 May 1948 positions seeded from the Phase five research file [S9] (`Q-START-4`, the owner's pick, 15 October 2026 [S4]; `D-G1`). Every row is fact-checked against its source before the `-shareable` tag (`Q-START-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). There are no air or naval units; where a phase needs them they are presentation-only (`Q-UNIT-5`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). Open mode starts with no units (`Q-START-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]).
- Orders are free and spend no Attention (`D-A6`). The standing orders are Move (תנועה), Go-to, Fortify (ביצור), Hold (החזקה) and Attack (התקפה) (`Q-UI-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S7]; owner, 23 October 2026, #1402 and #1398: "Approve, start building"). Fortify takes one turn, costs nothing, and grants a defence bonus (value: open (owner)); the bonus is lost the moment the unit moves (`Q-HOLD-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S7]). Hold can be given only on the player's own hex and grants no interception; its effect is a value: open (owner) (`Q-HOLD-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S7]).
- Movement costs integer movement points per unit type against an integer cost per biome; lakes and the sea are impassable (`Q-MOVE-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A unit with all its movement points may always enter one neighbouring land hex, whatever its cost; after spending points, it needs the full cost of the next hex (owner, 23 October 2026, #1402 and #1398: "No, strict like Civ VI" and "Approve, start building"). A built corridor road lowers its hex's movement cost, since roads mattered (`D-G2`; `Q-MOVE-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A stacking limit caps units of a side per hex; entering a hex an enemy unit holds is an attack (`Q-MOVE-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). There is no zone of control: a unit may enter any land hex it can pay for (`Q-MOVE-4`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A unit may not enter an enemy-held locality: entering would be a capture action, and the MVP records no rule for civilians under capture, so this is refused until Phase six (`Q-CIV-1`, the owner's pick, 15 October 2026 [S4]). There is no transport or embarkation (`Q-MOVE-5`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]).
- Combat follows the GDD: a deterministic table on the strength ratio, plus one RNG draw per engagement (`Q-COMBAT-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A unit attacks an adjacent enemy unit. A player's attack is one unit alone, fought the moment it is ordered; the enemy's attacks from several directions on one hex add their strengths; the defender's biome and fortify state modify the ratio (`Q-COMBAT-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]; owner, 23 October 2026, #1402 and #1398: "Instant, like Civ", "One unit per attack, Civ" and "Approve, start building"). Outcomes take losses from the table by population units; a losing defender retreats one hex; at strength zero a unit is removed; a defender with no retreat hex surrenders; the attacker does not advance into the empty hex in the MVP (`Q-COMBAT-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). Combat acts on combatant units only: enemy units on a hex with a locality's residents may be attacked, with no effect on the residents, and artillery is not excluded (`Q-CIV-2`, the owner's pick, 15 October 2026 [S4]; R-COMBAT-005; CONTENT_POLICY §4).
- Recruits arrive only through `Train` at a military camp (`docs/gdd/training.md` [S11]). Artillery and the armoured car become `Train` tracks at a military camp: a crew of civilians, plus an Arms and Money cost as design values; event grants are left to Phase seven (`Q-ARTY-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]; `D-H2`). Training a recruit costs Arms (a design value); an attack spends Arms per attacking unit, and a shortfall refuses with a reason (`D-G6`; `Q-UNIT-3` and `Q-COMBAT-4`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). There is no Arms upkeep; a unit costs Money upkeep each month (a design value) (`Q-UNIT-3`). A damaged formation standing in a friendly city can be refilled with trained replacements from that city's civilians, each group paying its course's Money and Arms, up to the strength the formation started with; a veteran stays a veteran, and the replacements are cancelled if the unit is lost or leaves the city [S17] (`R-TRAIN-004`).
- The player's artillery fires alone at an enemy unit within its range; it never holds ground, and firing it spends Arms (`Q-ARTY-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]; owner, 23 October 2026, #1402 and #1398: "One unit per attack, Civ" and "Approve, start building"). The armoured car carries more movement points and its own combat factor (`Q-ARTY-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). Artillery's on-screen name is Artillery (ארטילריה) until a sourced gun type arrives (`Q-ARTY-4`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]); which vehicles and guns are modelled stays open (`Q-ART-2`).
- Enemy forces act in the first resolver step after the player's orders (impact spec §2.3 [S8]; owner, 23 October 2026, #1402 and #1398: "Approve, start building"). In Historical mode they follow a script from two-source positions at 15 May and 11 June 1948 [S9], then act on simple goals; in Open mode goals only (`Q-ENEMY-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A goal is: hold a fortified position, or attack an adjacent enemy unit when the strength ratio meets a threshold; no RNG (`Q-ENEMY-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). Enemy armies receive no reinforcements until a dated per-army figure is sourced (`Q-ENEMY-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). Enemy units need no supply (`Q-ENEMY-4`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). The four operation cards, `ten_days`, `operation_yoav`, `operation_hiram`, `operation_horev`, give orders to units already on the board, and that resets their sensitivity review; which units, whose, and which orders: value: open (owner) (`Q-ENEMY-5`, the owner's pick, 15 October 2026 [S4]).
- The war state is what war events set; in the GDD game loop the security order and call-up are refused with their reason (`Q-WAR-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]; `D-C4`). The clock that forced war after fifty-two weeks from March 1948 is retired (`Q-WAR-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). During a truce attacks are refused with "a truce is in force"; movement is allowed. The refusal lasts from the first_truce acceptance until the turn covering 8 July 1948, and from the second_truce acceptance until the turn covering 15 October 1948 — the dates the fighting resumed in the MFA chronology [S10] (`Q-WAR-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). The `set_rung` command keeps acting outside the GDD loop (`Q-WAR-4`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A war loss is enemy units holding a player's GDD city hex at the end of a turn (`Q-WAR-5`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]); Phase 7 turns it into the defeat ending.
- Prisoners are the only captives, under the impact plan's §4.3 as written (`Q-POW-1`, the owner's pick, 15 October 2026 [S4]; impact spec §4.3 [S8]): only enemy combatant units can be captured, when an attack forces a surrender; residents can never be captured. Kills and enemy casualties never score (impact spec §4.3 [S8]). The bonus comes at the return, not the capture: prisoners are held with Food upkeep, are never labour, Money or a bargaining resource, and any loss while they are held is a cost; the wording frames the bonus as ending fighting with fewer lives lost, and nothing on screen celebrates the capture. A returned population unit scores a fixed number of points (points per unit: value: open (owner)) (`Q-POW-2`, the owner's pick, 15 October 2026 [S4]). The return happens on a sourced event card, one per front — the Egypt card carries 9 March 1949, the end of the main exchange (the owner's ruling, 16 October 2026 [S12]) — and the prisoners' return is never the player's action (`Q-POW-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S5]). The player's captured soldiers are counted, are returned at the exchanges, and cost while held (kind and amount: value: open (owner)), with no score effect (`Q-POW-4`, decided 15 October 2026 [S5]). Each prisoner costs Food per turn (value: open (owner)); a shortfall costs Social cohesion (cost: value: open (owner)) and never reduces the number of prisoners (`Q-POW-5`, decided 15 October 2026 [S5]; R-POW-002).
- The war objective completes on the last armistice (`D-B2`): one hundred points, plus five points for every month earlier than the last armistice's historical date, capped at sixty points (`D-B2` and `D-B6`; impact plan §5 [S8]). The fronts follow the agreements' parties: Egypt, Lebanon, Syria, and Jordan with Iraq (the Jordan agreement takes over the Iraqi sector, Art. VI); the Arab Liberation Army and the local forces belong to no front (`Q-OBJ-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). Each front's armistice card has its hard anchor at the agreement's date; it may open early as a soft event once the first turn on or after 16 November 1948 is reached on which that front has been quiet for a number of turns (labelled design value; the Security Council resolution of 16 November 1948 [S2]). A refused card returns when the front is quiet again (`Q-OBJ-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). The completion card's event is the Syria armistice of 20 July 1949 [S2]; if no suitably licensed archival photograph is found, the card shows no photograph; the card's losses line reports the Israeli total only (the total: value: open (owner) — one sourced Israeli value, checked by a human before it is marked fact-checked, CONTENT_POLICY §1 and §2.6; no other side's total) (`Q-OBJ-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]; `Q-CIV-4` as re-ruled, 15 October 2026 [S6]). On a front's armistice, that front's units leave the board, the player's units are free to move, and the armistice lines stay display-only (`Q-OBJ-4`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). Losses are shown as people per engagement, in the Why line, in the same words for both sides (`Q-CIV-4`, the owner's first ruling, 15 October 2026 [S4]). In Phase five no military action moves International Support or Social cohesion (`Q-CIV-3`, the owner's pick, 15 October 2026 [S4]); the prisoners' Food-shortfall cost to Social cohesion is the one exemption (`Q-POW-5`; R-POW-002, exempted under R-WAR-004 [S2]). Kills are never a score or a resource (impact spec §4.3 [S8]; CONTENT_POLICY §2).
- The attack preview shows the strength ratio and the table's outcome bands for it, from a sim helper (`Q-UI-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A token shows three figures at two thirds or more of full strength, two at a third or more, one below that (`Q-UI-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A side with no sourced flag draws a neutral pennant until a sourced emblem is found (`Q-ART-1`, the owner's pick, 15 October 2026 [S5]).
- Objective 1 also gives a bonus when a player unit reaches the Umm Rashrash hex before the war ends; this does not change when the war objective completes. The card is the Ink Flag, 10 March 1949 (Operation Uvda), sourced with `checked:` dates and a person's fact check. The points are a labelled design value for the balance pass; no value is selected here. **Then decided (owner, 18 Oct 2026, "Bonus inside the war"; [#1007](https://github.com/eyalgolan/statecraft-israel/issues/1007#issuecomment-6254333758))** [S15]. (unitless)

## Out of scope

- Settlement capture and control — Phase 6 (`Q-CIV-1` refuses entry until then).
- Which vehicles and guns are modelled — `Q-ART-2`, open; PR 17's sourcing.
- Open mode beyond its starting forces (`Q-START-2`).
- Demobilisation and return to civilian life — Phase 7 (`Q-UNIT-6`).
- Enemy reinforcements — `Q-ENEMY-3`.
- The defeat ending itself — Phase 7 (`D-B5`); this phase produces only the war-loss signal.
- Unit and card art, and the sensitivity review of it — PR 17; the review is a named reviewer plus the owner's sign-off (`Q-ART-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S5]).

## Open questions

The amounts the rows leave open are proposed as labelled design values in each implementing PR and accepted there (`Q-GEN-1`): the combat table values, movement points per type, the fortify and hold effects, the points per returned prisoner unit, the kind and amount of the cost of the player's held soldiers, Food per prisoner and the cohesion cost of a shortfall, the completion card's Israeli total, and the quiet-turn count before an armistice card may open early. The Egypt prisoner card's date is no longer open: 9 March 1949 (the owner's ruling, 16 October 2026 [S12]). `Q-ART-2` (which vehicles and guns) stays open for PR 17's sourcing; the `Q-ART-3` reviewer is unnamed (owner).
