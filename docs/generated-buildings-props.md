## Editor review and validation

The review scene lives under `Assets/Editor/Generated1948Review/`, is absent from build settings and has no runtime loader. Its component is guarded by `UNITY_EDITOR`. Use **Statecraft → Generated 1948 → Prepare buildings and props**, then **Play buildings and props review**. Preparation validates lossless imports and sets up only editor prefabs/scene references. It never appends a catalog entry, writes TownModels.asset or generates LODs.

The [QA receipt](generated-buildings-props-qa.json) records current verification and remaining gates. For the 24 October regeneration, each new geometry passed a single-sided opening check before it was textured: rays cast from above, from four sides and from four elevated diagonals, and they find no roof or wall opening beyond doorway and window recesses and the open porch. Rear views were 11.2% see-through for the old 03 and are 0.0% now. Top views of 01 were 2.0% see-through and are 0.0% now, and 05's roof deck is closed over both wings. Hits that remain are well under 1% from the elevated views and come from open doorway or window recesses and the 01 porch, which is open by design. The FBX conversion keeps triangle positions, connectivity and UVs, and its stored corner normals match the source exactly. For 03, Blender's reimport reports a 1.0 normal difference on a degenerate face; Unity imports the stored normals, so this does not affect the review. Unity 6000.3.25f1 EditMode `Generated1948AssetTests|Generated1948BuildingsPropsTests` passed 5/5 with no compiler warnings or errors, covering actual import and settings, source hashes, triangle counts, player dependency isolation and layout. `tools/tests/test_generated_buildings_props_package.py` passes. Local Editor Play Mode evidence is in `captures/buildings-props-regenerated-review-v3/`: `all11-overview.png`, `regenerated-overview.jpg`, and front and rear views of the three regenerated houses in `regenerated-front-rear-contact.jpg`. Previous original-source captures stay in `captures/buildings-props-originals-review-v2/` for comparison. That 24 October verification claimed no player/WebGL result and no art approval; the owner's sign-off of all eleven followed on 27 October 2026 (#756, "Sign off all 19").
