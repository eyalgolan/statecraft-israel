# GDD v0.1 Phase 3: cities and absorption at MVP scale. Implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (one PR at a time, inline, as in Phases 1 and 2). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. **The owner reviews this plan before any code.** Every PR below names the owner questions it waits on; a PR does not start until its questions are answered and the answers are recorded (PR 0).

**Status (15 October 2026):** this plan merged as #573 and is the plan of record for Phase 3. The Phase 2 plan merged as #568. PR 0 below holds the recommended answers to every open question, for the owner to decide.

**Goal:** Take the one-city slice to the MVP's twelve Jewish cities and towns: census start values, the CBS arrivals spread over the cities, housing capacity and condition with overflow, city projects (housing, industry, one specialised industry) with a build time and a Money cost, the craftsman, and the "absorb immigrants" objective with its sourced milestone.

**Architecture:** Phase 1 built one city (`Cities.cs`, R-CITY-001 to R-CITY-005) and the resolver (`TurnResolver.cs`). Phase 2 turns the GDD loop on for the real game and moves the Phase 1 balance values into `content/` as labelled design values (#568, Q-CUT-1). Phase 3 fills `content/cities.yaml` with the twelve cities, makes arrivals land across them, adds housing condition and overflow to step 8 of the resolver, and adds city projects to step 5 (construction) beside the pioneer work of Phase 1 PR 5 (`Projects.cs`). It retires the legacy map actions (`MapActions.cs`) into projects, and adds an objective to the Phase 1 PR 6 objective machinery. After cutover every PR that changes an MVP rule moves the goldens, re-pins them in the same PR and says why (spec §3, "After cutover").

**Tech stack:** as Phases 1 and 2: C# netstandard2.1 sim (`sim/Statecraft.Sim`), xUnit on net10.0 (`sim/Statecraft.Sim.Tests`), YamlDotNet content, Unity 6000.3.25f1 UI Toolkit (`unity/`), Python tools and policy checks under `uv`.

**Spec:** [the impact plan](../specs/2026-10-12-gdd-v0.1-impact-and-plan.md), §1.2 (the Housing row), §1.4, §2.2, §2.3, §4.4 and §6 "Phase 3"; its §5 group D rows and D-B4. ## Review focus

1. **People lost or doubled when arrivals are spread.** Over any run, the refugees added to all cities equal `floor(P(end) / people_per_unit) - floor(P(start) / people_per_unit)`, whatever the split. A per-city, per-turn rounding loses units; the split must be cumulative. (PR 2.)
2. **The national head count and the cities disagree silently.** After PR 2 there is one stated relation between `GameState.Population` and the cities (Q-ARRV-2), checked by a test, and the CBS anchors still hold. (PR 2.)
3. **A value that claims to be historical without a source, or a design value that reads as history.** Every field in `cities.yaml` has a status (`sourced`, `derived`, `wikipedia-only` or `design`), a source for the first three, and the census date where it applies; the design-value listing test names every design field. (PR 1.)
4. **Overflow that makes people vanish or appear.** People beyond housing capacity are still counted somewhere the player can see, and a turn never changes a city's total people except by arrivals, training and the stated rules. (PR 3.)
5. **A project that pays twice or refunds on cancel.** Money is paid once (at the start or by turns, Q-PROJ-1), a cancelled project returns its people and not its Money (D-A3), and a completed project adds its capacity or slots exactly once. (PR 4.)
6. **The objective completes on arrival, not absorption,** or completes from people housed in a way §4.1 forbids. (PR 7.)
