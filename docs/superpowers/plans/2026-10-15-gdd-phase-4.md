# GDD v0.1 Phase 4: pioneers, designated sites and corridors. Implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (one PR at a time, inline, as in Phases 1 to 3). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. **The owner reviews this plan before any code.** Every PR below names the owner questions it waits on. A PR does not start until those questions are answered and the answers are recorded (PR 0).

**Status (15 October 2026):** a proposed plan, in review. PR 0 below holds a recommended answer for every open question, for the owner to decide.

**Goal:** All six of the GDD's pioneer actions, at designated sites and along designated corridors: build road, build railroad, plow fields, dry swamp, build yishuv and build a training camp. `sites.yaml` holds the MVP's sites and `corridors.yaml` its corridors, with progress per segment, and `RouteLayer` draws a corridor from that progress. A halutz unit becomes a veteran pioneer. Objective 3, "Establish key infrastructure", completes in three parts: the Tel Aviv to Haifa railway, the Burma Road corridor to Jerusalem (DECISIONS G1), and the new yishuvim. Each part fires its sourced card.

**Architecture:** Phase 1 PR 5 (#566, open) adds `content/sites.yaml` with one site (Mefalsim), the command `PioneerWork(unit, action, target)` with `StopWork(unit)`, and construction as step 5 of `TurnResolver` (`Projects.cs`, R-SITE-001 to R-SITE-003). Phase 3 PR 4 adds city projects to the same step. Phase 4 adds five more actions to the same command, and keeps one project per pioneer unit (D-D6). It adds a sim-read `content/corridors.yaml` (id, kind, segments, each an ordered list of hexes, sources) and a new `Corridors.cs`. The state holds only the progress of each segment (spec §2.2: `Corridor: segment progress[] (int)`). Route geometry stays map-only: it is traced by `tools/routes_1948.py` into `route_paths_1948.yaml`, baked into the ribbon file, and drawn by `RouteLayer` through the route index of #436. A plowed field adds Food in step 6 to Phase 2's national pool. After cutover every PR that changes an MVP rule moves the goldens, re-pins them in the same PR and says why (spec §3, "After cutover").

**Tech stack:** as Phases 1 to 3: C# netstandard2.1 sim (`sim/Statecraft.Sim`), xUnit on net10.0 (`sim/Statecraft.Sim.Tests`), YamlDotNet content, Unity 6000.3.25f1 UI Toolkit (`unity/`), Python tools and policy checks under `uv`.

Facts found while writing this plan:
- **The GDD's railway opened after the MVP.** The new coastal line from Remez Junction to Tel Aviv North opened officially on 14 May 1953 (corroborated by *The Jerusalem Post* of 26 April and 14 May 1953, and by the *Jewish Chronicle* of 22 May 1953; corridors note, Corroboration row 1). Inside the window, Haifa to Tel Aviv passenger service by rail began on **20 September 1949**. The route ran over the old Eastern Railway, by a new bypass west of Tulkarm, reversed at Rosh HaAyin, and ran over a new 7 km link along the Yarkon to a new Tel Aviv North station. Agam confirmed the day in the press on #384: *Haboker*, 14 September 1949, and *Maariv*, 22 September 1949. Of that route, only the bypass and the 7 km link were new track. That is D-B3 (Q-RAIL-1 and Q-RAIL-2).
- **The Burma Road was finished on 14 June 1948, one turn after the start.** The decided card date, 7 December 1948 (#384, G1), is the opening of the Road of Valour (*Hayom*, 8 December 1948). The Road of Valour was a separate permanent road: 40.5 km, by Nahshon and Shimshon junctions to Sha'ar HaGai. `routes_1948.yaml` does not have it. docs/gdd/corridors.md already words the card as "the Road of Valour, which replaced the Burma Road track". Which line the player builds is Q-COR-1.
- **The corridors are short on this map.** Hex centres are about 8.7 km apart (docs/units.md). The Yarkon link is about one hex, and the Burma Road from Dayr Muhaysin to Saris is about two. The Tel Aviv to Netanya coastal road (26 km, *Jerusalem Post*, 30 July 1950) is about three. So segments follow the sources' named waypoints, not a count per hex (Q-COR-2).
- **No existing route is a new build.** Every route in `routes_1948.yaml` except the Burma Road was on the 1946 map. The impact plan's D-D7 recommendation, "2–3 roads, from `routes_1948.yaml` sources", can therefore only pick roads that were built from 1948 to 1951 and that the corridors note found missing from the file (its comparison table, rows 5, 6, 11, 12, 14 to 19).
- **The `hula_drainage` event and a "dry swamp" project can contradict each other.** If the player drained the Hula earlier, or never started, the event would describe works that are not there (impact plan §4.4). Q-SWAMP-2.
- **Training happens in cities, not at sites.** `Train(city, track, n)` reads a city's `facilities`. A camp built "at a designated site" (GDD) has to give some city that facility, or Train has to accept a site (Q-CAMP-1).
- **Two of the candidate sites have a 7 October 2023 association:** Mefalsim (#566, question 4) and Re'im (settlements note, row 25). #566 answered (a) for Mefalsim: the site covers only the founding.

## Review focus

1. **Building off a designated spot.** Every pioneer action names a site or a corridor segment from content. No command takes a free hex, and a site of one kind refuses another kind's action. (PRs 1 to 5.)
2. **A segment built twice, or a corridor complete with a gap.** A segment's progress reaches its turns once. A corridor is complete only when every segment is built, in any order. Two units never build the same segment. (PR 2.)
3. **The sim and the map disagree.** The hexes in `corridors.yaml` and the traced route agree (a CI test re-derives them). `RouteLayer` draws exactly the built segments, from sim progress, never from the date in the GDD loop. (PRs 2 and 8.)
4. **Food from nothing, or Food from a depopulated place.** A field yields only after it is plowed, by the stated amount, in step 6. No site is on a place §4.1 item 4 forbids. (PRs 1 and 4.)
5. **The Hula event describing works that do not exist.** The event fires only in a state where the player's works match its text. (PR 5.)
6. **The objective scoring twice, or a card that tells the wrong history.** Each part fires its card once. D-B6's 100 points are paid once. The railway card says what happened on 20 September 1949 (service restored), not that the coastal line was finished. (PR 7.)
