## Migration dependencies

Start with population identity, region/facility bindings and ruleset/save boundaries. Build reception/work and the national ledger on that model. Pioneers/military share those people/equipment; control/evacuation shares region ids. Events/cards/endings consume sim results rather than another presentation economy.

Existing phase paths remain, with current owners and immutable historical originals. Replan against the actual checkout; old hashes, line numbers and PR status are observations for the earlier commit.

## Superseded contracts to remove during implementation

- Attention, automatic borrowing and passive budget duplication.
- City-only census rounding and housing-only refugee conversion.
- Passive unstaffed fields, fixed-time city construction, service-time pioneer promotion and attack Arms spending.
- Any-city automatic defeat and direct Money-shortage effects beyond housing deterioration.
- Obsolete camp identity/capacity assumptions.

These labels identify migration work; feature owners define replacements.

## Review and validation

Retain [stable tested IDs](../../rules/mvp.md), migrate real data/tests, label defaults, review deterministic hash changes and preserve factual/licence/sensitivity gates. Only [the root register](../../GDD.md#deferred-decisions) lists open principles. No human sign-off is implied.
