# Procurement and the defence industry, 1957 to 1977

Researched 24 October 2026. Every URL below was opened on that date by an agent. **Research only:** a note for issue #124
(1957-1977 item 12, part of #50), the research part the owner opened on 24 Oct. It adds no event text, no project
definition and no number for a game file; nothing under `content/` is changed. The procurement content stays paused until
after the MVP.

## Purpose

For each procurement project the issue names: its dates, what each side said, and the source. The projects are France as
supplier until 1967 and its embargo, the United States from 1962, the Cherbourg boats, IAI's aircraft, the Merkava from
1970, and the Negev reactor (design §10.4, D8: the public record only). It also closes, where a source allows, the
delivery-date [GAP] of [forces-1967-73.md](forces-1967-73.md) section 6.1; dates that rest on press are [S], not verified. The design is
[the 1957-1977 era design](../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-era-1957-1977-design.md) (§2.4 rows on France, the US,
IAI and Merkava; §10.4; §12 item 12).

- **FRUS** read from the Office of the Historian's TEI files (https://github.com/HistoryAtState/frus, `volumes/<id>.xml`),
  the source of https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/<volume>/d<n>. Volumes read: 1958-60 XIII, 1961-63 XVII
  and XVIII, 1964-68 XVIII, XIX, XX, 1969-76 XXIII. Citations give volume and document number.
- **JTA archive** (https://www.jta.org/archive) through its own search API, with the original bulletin PDFs
  (pdfs.jta.org) for datelines. Where the page text of a PDF could not be OCR-read, the dateline was read from the
  rendered page image.
- **SIPRI Arms Transfers Database** (https://armstransfers.sipri.org): the page answered HTTP 200 on 24 Oct, but the
  register needs script-driven queries and none was run, so no SIPRI figure is used. A year-by-year Israeli import series
  is a [GAP].
- **Israeli tier 1 sources** (Knesset records, MoD, IDF Archives, gov.il) were not retried on 24 Oct; the previous
  notes in this folder record them as blocked or geo-blocked. Nothing here rests on them.
- **Not read:** SIPRI data, the UK National Archives, French archives, IAI's own history pages.
