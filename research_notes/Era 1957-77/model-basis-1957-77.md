## Purpose

[The 1957-1977 design](../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-era-1957-1977-design.md) section 5.1 lists the policies, indicators, the `recession` situation and the voter group that each "need a sourced historical basis before it lands", and marks some dates "to verify" (for example the start of `open_bridges`). Section 5.5 lists the actor windows, with the PLO's founding and UNDOF marked "to verify". This note gives, row by row, what a source says, in what words and on which day, where two sources differ, and what is still open. Where another note of this era already sources a fact, the row points to it and does not repeat it (the economy note for the slowdown's numbers in particular).

Rows of the design that are values and not history (the level a policy opens at, the hysteresis of a situation, the names under D10) have no source to find; the note says so instead of filling them.
