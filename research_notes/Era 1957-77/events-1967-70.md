## A. The waiting period and the Six-Day War, 15 May to July 1967

### A1. The waiting period opens: Egypt's alert and moves into Sinai (14-15 May 1967)

- **Date(s).** 14 and 15 May 1967 [V, agent read 2026-10-24; a person's check pending]. The design's 15 May opening and its "from 16 May" are both in
  forces-1967-73.md section 1 ([C] there: the US record puts the alert and the first moves on 14-15 May).
- **Summary.** Egypt put its armed forces on alert and began moving ground forces into Sinai. On 12 May Eshkol had
  warned publicly of "appropriate means" against Syria; the Egyptian moves followed. The Israeli intelligence estimate
  of these days (the "Aman estimate") is in forces-1967-73.md section 5.1 (see forces-1967-73.md): its own text is
  still a [GAP], its content comes through FRUS 64-68 v19 d61.
- **Sources.**
  - FRUS 1964-68, vol. XIX, doc. 297 (draft briefing by DCI Helms, Washington, 14 June 1967):
    https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v19/d297 . "On May 14 and 15, Nasir placed the Egyptian armed forces on “full alert,” and began moving ground forces into Sinai with much fanfare." Also "On May 12, Prime Minister Eshkol warned publicly that “if there is no other way,” Israel would have to use “appropriate means” to punish the Syrians for terrorist acts." [V, agent read 2026-10-24; a person's check pending]
  - FRUS 64-68 v19 d2 (Tel Aviv, 15 May 1967): quoted in forces-1967-73.md section 1.
- **Gaps.** [GAP] Israeli mobilisation day by day and its cost to the economy (design §6, "the mobilisation's daily cost"): no Israeli source reached; the CIA's figure of "at least 40 to 50 percent mobilization" on 23 May is in forces-1967-73.md section 5.1.

### A2. UNEF is asked to leave and goes (16 to about 23 May 1967); Sinai remilitarised

- **Date(s).**
  - 16 May 1967, 10 p.m. Gaza time: a letter from the Egyptian Chief of Staff, Fawzi, to UNEF's commander Rikhye asks UNEF to withdraw from the observation posts along the border (FRUS d6) [V, agent read 2026-10-24; a person's check pending].
  - 16 May, 6:45 p.m. (New York): the Secretary-General's reply that a request to withdraw UNEF must come directly from the Egyptian government and that he would then order the withdrawal of all UNEF troops (FRUS d6) [V, agent read 2026-10-24; a person's check pending].
  - 18 May: U Thant's report to the General Assembly on the withdrawal, per the FRUS editorial note (d6) [V, agent read 2026-10-24; a person's check pending]. The day he gave the order is not in the documents read [GAP].
  - 19 May: the US memorandum that the report shows U Thant took the line that he "had no alternative but to order it to leave" (FRUS d19) [V, agent read 2026-10-24; a person's check pending].
  - By 23 May the withdrawal was complete: "who by this time had accomplished the complete withdrawal of the United Nations Emergency Force" (d297) [V, agent read 2026-10-24; a person's check pending]. The day UNEF left Sharm el-Sheikh and the day Egyptian forces took it are not in the documents read [GAP].
  - [C] The design gives "16 / 19 May" (en.wiki Six-Day War and UNEF articles). The US documents read give 16 May for the request and 18-19 May for the Secretary-General's report; none read gives 19 May as the day UNEF left.
- **Summary.** Egypt asked UNEF to pull back from its border posts; the Secretary-General answered that it was all of UNEF or none and withdrew all of it. The US thought he went faster and further than Nasser asked: on 26 May the CIA wrote that Nasser "probably did not expect such a speedy departure of UN forces from Sharm El Sheikh". Sinai, demilitarised since 1957, was filled with Egyptian forces.

[V, agent read 2026-10-24; a person's check pending]

### A3. The Straits of Tiran are closed (announced early 23 May 1967)

- **Date(s).** Early on 23 May, Cairo time (FRUS d297) [V, agent read 2026-10-24; a person's check pending]. Eshkol's statement in the Knesset that blocking the Straits would be an act of war: the JTA bulletin of 24 May (the item's dateline did not come out legibly in the OCR; the page carries items datelined 23 May) [S].
- **Summary.** Nasser announced that Egypt was again closing the Gulf of Aqaba to Israeli-flag shipping and to other ships carrying strategic material to Eilat. The CIA noted that the Israelis would consider this a justifiable cause for war. Israel had said on 19 May (A2) that interference at Sharm el-Sheikh would have "grave consequences".

### A5. The unity government; Dayan becomes Minister of Defence (25 May to 4 June 1967)

- **Date(s).**
  - 25 May: negotiations "have reached the penultimate stage"; Rafi and Gahal to get two ministers each [S: JTA, dateline JERUSALEM, 25 May].
  - 31 May: talks to widen the Cabinet resumed; strong opposition in Mapam and Mapai [S: JTA, dateline TEL AVIV, 31 May].
  - The night of 31 May: the Cabinet approved Eshkol's plan, "which calls for appointment of Labor Minister Yigal Allon as Defense Minister", on condition that Dayan be offered and accept a major post [S: JTA, item datelined TEL AVIV, 1 June, "last night"].
  - By 2 June, 11:30 a.m. Washington time: the US Secretary of State's meeting already speaks of "The appointment of Moshe Dayan as Defense Minister" (FRUS d130) [V, agent read 2026-10-24; a person's check pending]; 3 June: the popular applause "go, go Moshe" (FRUS d143) [V, agent read 2026-10-24; a person's check pending].
  - 4 June: the enlarged Cabinet met with "the newly-designated Minister of Defense, Moshe Dayan" and with Begin and Sapir of Gahal [S: JTA, dateline JERUSALEM, 4 June].
  - [C] The design's "1, 2 and 5 June" for Dayan's start (en.wiki, he.wiki). The sources read show him designated by 2 June; the Knesset's approval and the swearing-in are not in them [GAP].
- **Summary.** Pressure for a national unity Cabinet grew during the waiting period. Eshkol's first plan gave the defence portfolio to Allon; Mapai branches, above all Haifa's, pressed for Dayan; Dayan reportedly preferred an army post under Rabin but became Defence Minister. Gahal (Begin, Sapir) and Rafi (Dayan, Peres) joined. The US read the appointment as a sign against restraint.

### A8. Jerusalem: the Old City, the Moroccan Quarter and the boundary (7 June to 14 July 1967)

- **Date(s).**
  - 7 June: the Old City and the Western Wall taken; the first Jewish prayers at the Wall "captured earlier in the day by Israeli troops and armor" (JTA item datelined OLD CITY OF JERUSALEM, 7 June) [S]. Its military account is in forces-1967-73.md section 7.1.
  - 10 June, Saturday evening: the Moroccan (Mughrabi) Quarter's demolition begins, according to Wikipedia's article, which quotes an eyewitness; 135 houses and "at least 650 people" made refugees [S]. The design gives 10 June.
  - 16 June: Rusk told Harman he "understood Knesset might be thinking of imminent action to take over old Jerusalem" (FRUS d303) [V, agent read 2026-10-24; a person's check pending].
  - 27 June: the Knesset approved three bills extending Israeli law, jurisdiction and administration to the Old City and other eastern areas (FRUS d333) [V, agent read 2026-10-24; a person's check pending].
