Research only, 2026-10-31. Refs #118. This note gives, for each candidate named in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-era-1957-1977-design.md` (section 10.3 rule 3, the tagged events of section 10.2, and Dimona in section 10.4), the date and one sourced fact. It proposes no tag and no include, omit or how-to-show call: those stay with the owner (issue #118, owner comments of 22 and 31 October). It changes no content file, test or code. Tone follows `docs/CONTENT_POLICY.md`.

## Conventions

- **[V, agent read DATE; a person's check pending]**: the primary or contemporaneous record was opened and the fact is in it. Every [V] here is an agent's reading, not a person's check.
- **[S, ...]**: a single secondary source (Wikipedia by oldid permalink, or similar), named.
- **[C, ...]**: sources give different values; every value is listed with its source. No value is chosen.
- **[G]**: not found, with the search recorded. (No row is [G] in this pass.)
- JTA archive pages show the bulletin date; where the event date was read from the dateline of the original bulletin PDF, the row says so. Several JTA scans have no text layer and were read by OCR; the row says when a date rests on wording inside the text instead of the dateline.
- Casualty figures are given only as a source gives them. Where a design table (section 2.3) gives a different date or count, both appear in the row.
