# Development towns founded 1957-1977 inside the pre-1967 lines (research note, 2026-10-25)

Status: research only. Nothing here is in `content/`, `sim/`, `tools/` or `unity/`; the dated settlements file (#61)
stays paused until after the MVP. Refs #130 (1957-77 18), the "Settlements" row of
[the 1957-1977 design](../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-era-1957-1977-design.md) section 7.3: "Development towns
(Arad, Karmiel and others; founding dates *to verify*)". One topic of #130 only. **Kibbutzim, moshavim and settlements
in the territories are not in this note**, and neither are the lines. Which rows enter the game, and their names, wait
for the owner's sensitivity list (#118); this note makes no include or omit choice and no naming choice.

It follows the format of [`settlements-1949-51.md`](../GDD%20objectives/settlements-1949-51.md) and of the 1952-56
notes in `research_notes/Map/` (which already hold the development towns founded before 1957; those are not repeated
here). Every page below was opened on **25 October 2026** unless a row says otherwise.

The question: which development towns were founded between 1 January 1957 and 31 December 1977 inside Israel's
pre-1967 lines, and for each: founding date (and what the date marks), coordinates from a named source, and sources.

## Short answer

- **There is no official list of development towns.** The Knesset Research and Information Center looked for one in
  2018 and wrote that it did not find one (section "Which list", below). So membership here is built from two stated
  lists, and the choice is the owner's.
- On the list the Knesset Center itself used (25 towns, the Central Bureau of Statistics list of April 2018), **four
  towns have a founding year of 1957-1977 in the official CBS localities file**: Ma'alot (part of Ma'alot-Tarshiha,
  1957), Nof HaGalil (called Nazareth Illit until 2019, 1957), Arad (1961) and Karmiel (1964). The other 21 have a CBS
  year of 1956 or earlier, or are older towns.
- **All four have disagreements on the year or the day**, kept below as [C]: other sources say 1956 for Ma'alot, 1955
  or 1956 for Nazareth Illit, 1960 or 1962 for Arad.
- No other row of the CBS file with a city or local-council status and a 1957-1977 year is on either list (section "Not
  on either list"); four such rows are not on a list and are not in the territories, and the note does not call them
  development towns.

### The window rule used

A town is "in the window" when the CBS year of founding is 1957 to 1977 and it is inside the pre-1967 lines. For those
towns the row says what each source's date marks (a planning decision, a first family, a ceremony, a merger, a local
council). Where a different source puts the founding in 1956 or earlier, that stays visible as [C]; it does not move
the town out of the table.

  - Hebrew Wikipedia, section on the founding **[S, Wikipedia]**: "ידיעה ראשונה בעיתונות על הקמת היישוב היהודי מעלות
