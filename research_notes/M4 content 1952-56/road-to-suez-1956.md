# M4 content research: the road to Suez and the Sinai Campaign (June to November 1956)

Researched 2026-10-11 and re-checked 2026-10-15. Draft for issue #5: the Suez note that `events-1952-56.md` (A11) says is planned. That file's A9, A10, A11, B10 and B11 cover the same weeks in shorter form; this note keeps their dates and says where it adds to them or differs. The M4 plan names "Suez (1956)" as an anchor. The war-mode work (#103) and event drafting (#28) can use the dates below.

This note is research, not a design. It adds no event text and changes no content file.

**Purpose.** Give the dates, the people in office, the secret French and British dealings, the Israeli decision and the war and ceasefire anchors for the Sinai Campaign (Operation Kadesh), each tagged by how well it is sourced.

**Conventions** (the same as the other M4 notes)
- **[V]** verified on a page opened on 2026-10-15 (URL given).
- **[S]** single source, a Wikipedia-only fact, a search-result summary that was not opened, or a page read on 2026-10-11 that could not be re-opened on 2026-10-15.
- **[C]** sources conflict. Every version is given.
- **[GAP]** not found.
- No quote is invented. The few short phrases in quotation marks are copied from the cited text; everything else is paraphrased. Wikipedia is never [V].
- **JTA dates.** A JTA bulletin is dated the day after its dispatches (see `new-economic-policy-1952.md`). The JTA web text drops datelines and the 1956 scans would not open here, so a JTA "today" is not pinned to a day unless another source confirms it.

**Source caveat.** No Knesset debate record, Israeli cabinet protocol or Israel State Archives file was opened. The cabinet protocols are the key primary source for section 4 and are a [GAP]. The strongest sources opened on 2026-10-15 are FRUS 1955-57 vol XVI (documents 1, 373, 413, 414, 521 and 525), the Israel MoD (IDF and Defence Establishment) Archive exhibition page, the gov.il Seventh Government and Sinai Campaign pages, the Knesset open data record for ministers' posts, the UK National Archives page on the Dean memorandum (Wayback copy), the Hansard page for 30 Oct 1956 and two JTA bulletins. The gov.il chronology ("Highlights of main events 1947 to 1974") returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-15 and a Wayback copy did not load, so every row that rests only on it is tagged [S] and says so.

## 3. Sèvres, 22 to 24 Oct 1956

| Fact | Status and source |
|---|---|

On 24 Oct the document was signed by Ben-Gurion, Pineau and Patrick Dean, who attended the final talks in Lloyd's place | [V] FRUS 1955-57 vol XVI, doc 373, editorial note: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1955-57v16/d373 |

Israel occupies the western shore of the Gulf of Aqaba and the islands of Tiran and Sanafir, and promises not to attack Jordan | [V] FRUS doc 373 (above). ## 4. The Israeli decision

| Date | Fact | Status and source |
|---|---|---|

**Options on the table, and who argued for each**
- **Wait or use diplomacy.** The nearest record is Sharett's removal on 18 to 19 Jun. The NYT headline snippet gives the reason as the Foreign Minister being too cautious and temporizing; this is a headline snippet, [S]. A scholarly article on the sacking exists: [S] https://mej.mei.edu/content/mej/70/2/275.full.pdf (not opened). [GAP] for any named minister who argued for waiting in the Oct 1956 debate.
- **Preventive war, Gaza first.** Dayan (end of 1955), per the MoD page [V].
- **Preventive war, Tiran first.** Ben-Gurion (end of 1955), per the MoD page [V]. In July 1956 Dayan advised attacking at the first chance while Ben-Gurion preferred French backing: [S] Wikipedia "Suez Crisis".
- **Join the Anglo-French plan.** Ben-Gurion kept the plan from most ministers except a few, including Meir (MoD page [V]); Dayan and Peres were at Sèvres (FRUS doc 373 [V]).
- **Cabinet doubters.** [GAP]. Do not name anyone without the protocols or a memoir read in full.

## 6. Sensitive events in the same window (pointer only)

Kafr Qasim (29 Oct 1956) and Kalkilya (10 to 11 Oct 1956) occurred within these weeks, as did the killings in Khan Yunis and Rafah during Kadesh (3 Nov and 12 Nov 1956). They are not described here.

- **Kafr Qasim** is B11 in `events-1952-56.md`: `massacre` (D8 rule 3, react-only), death toll [C] 43 to 49.
- **Kalkilya** is B10 in that file (D8 rule 4 candidate). B10 already has the launch time (about nine in the evening) and the figures: Jordan's 40 dead, an Israeli estimate of more than 100 Jordanian military dead, and Israel's 16 dead, two missing and 12 wounded. This note adds one number from the same JTA bulletin of 12 Oct 1956: UN observers were shown 48 Jordanian bodies and two Israeli soldiers' bodies, a fourth figure for B10's casualty [C]. [V] https://www.jta.org/archive/israelis-destroy-jordanian-stronghold-in-seven-hour-battle . The night of 10 to 11 Oct follows from the JTA dating rule [S].
- **The killings in Khan Yunis and Rafah during Kadesh** (Nov 1956) are not covered in `events-1952-56.md` (line 104), which leaves them to this note. The dates, 3 Nov (Khan Yunis) and 12 Nov 1956 (Rafah camp), come from the UNRWA Director's special report, A/3212/Add.1, paragraphs 23 and 24, read on 11 Oct 2026 as reproduced at https://www.palquest.org/en/historictext/9611/special-report-director-unrwa (the UN record, https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/710955, was not opened). Not re-opened on 15 Oct, so they are [S]. Kafr Qasim's date comes from the Akevot Institute (https://www.akevot.org.il/en/news-item/now-online-comprehensive-document-library-on-the-1956-kafr-qasim-massacre/, read 11 Oct 2026). The Khan Yunis and Rafah killings need D8 rule 4 proposals for the owner before any event is drafted.

## 7. Proposed event shape (for #28, not drafted)

- **Period:** 22 to 29 Oct 1956, in the window after Sèvres and before the invasion.
- **Decision-maker:** `{prime_minister}`, with `{defense_minister}` and `{foreign_minister}` named in text. In the game's model Ben-Gurion holds the prime-minister and defence roles; the foreign minister is the officeholder from 19 Jun 1956 (Knesset open data).
- **Choices that the record supports:** accept the Anglo-French plan and launch; hold back and press diplomacy; act alone on Tiran or Gaza. Only the first happened. Treat the other two as counterfactual and label them as such.
- **War mode (#21):** anchors in section 5 only; the Tiran closure start year stays out of the text.
- **Golden hashes:** every date in the proposed event window (section 7) is after 27 Feb 1952, so an event built from it moves only the planned Hash1957 and not the Hash1952 golden.
