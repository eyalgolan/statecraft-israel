# Umm Rashrash and the Ink Flag, 10 March 1949: sources, location, card text and picture (research note, AI draft, 2026-10-20)

Status: **AI draft, not checked by a person.** Research only: nothing here changes `content/`, `content/map/`,

## Scope

The owner's ruling (#1007, 18 October 2026; `docs/GDD.md`, "Owner rulings, 2026-10-17", item 2; `docs/gdd/military-combat.md`
[S15]): objective 1 (#752) gives a bonus when a player unit reaches the Umm Rashrash hex before the war ends, and this
does not change when the war objective completes. The card is the Ink Flag, 10 March 1949 (Operation Uvda). This note
covers only what the offer asks for:

1. a source/claim table for Operation Uvda, the Ink Flag and the 10 March 1949 date;
2. a location proposal for Umm Rashrash at the precision each source gives (a proposal only, no map edit);
3. draft card text;
4. a picture that the policy allows, with its real licence, date and credit.

It does not touch the mechanic, the points (a labelled design value for the balance pass, #813) or when the war ends
(D-B2, the last armistice). It reuses [objective-cards.md](objective-cards.md) section 1 as leads, not as a fact check.

**The date.** 10 March 1949 rests on a UN document of 13 March 1949 (row 1). There the day comes from a UN observer
whose source was mainly the Arab Legion; the Israeli confirmation it reports covers the occupation, and the text does
not say whether it also covers the day. Every secondary source read gives the same day. No source read gives another day for reaching Umm
Rashrash or for the flag. (A Jewish Agency page seen in a search result gives 13 March for the flag [L]; it was not
read and stands alone.)

**Sensitivity, for the reviewer.** Zochrot's Nakba Map lists Umm al-Rashrash as a locality with "Population 1948: 50",
Beersheba district, occupied 10 March 1949 by the Negev and Golani brigades, "without a battle" (photo caption)
(https://www.zochrot.org/villages/village_details/48998/en). S/1286 also reports "numerous refugees moving from the
Palestine area across the border into the town of Aqaba". The draft card below claims neither an empty place nor a
battle. The water's name is a choice: S/1286 says "Gulf of Aqaba", Israeli sources "Gulf of Eilat"; the draft uses the
Red Sea, as NLI's titles do.

## 3. Draft card text (EN)

```yaml
title: The Ink Flag at Umm Rashrash
text: 'On the afternoon of 10 March 1949, at the end of Operation Uvda, soldiers of the Negev Brigade reached the abandoned police post at Umm Rashrash on the Red Sea. They drew a flag in ink on a white sheet and raised it over the post. Golani arrived about two hours later. The city of Eilat later grew on the site.'
```

Each clause and its row: the date and "afternoon" (rows 1, 3); "end of Operation Uvda" (row 8); the Negev Brigade
first (row 3); abandoned police post (row 4); a flag drawn in ink on a sheet (row 5); Golani about two hours later (rows 3, 7);
Eilat on the site (en.wiki, JVL, he.wiki). Left out on purpose: whose post it was (row 4), who drew the flag (row 5),
"ended the war" (row 9), numbers of men (row 2, Arab Legion second hand) and Ben-Gurion's "midnight" (row 10).

No Hebrew draft: the earlier research notes give no Hebrew card text, and the event YAMLs carry none. The Hebrew card
text is for the native reviewer (#564).
