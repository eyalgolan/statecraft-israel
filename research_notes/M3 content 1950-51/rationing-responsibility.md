# Who held rationing responsibility from 8 October 1951?
Sources checked 11 October 2026. Research for statecraft-israel issue #2.

## Decision
Add Dov Yosef as the minister responsible for rationing from 1951-10-08. This was food-supply and rationing responsibility within Trade and Industry, not a revival of the abolished Ministry of Supply and Rationing. Confidence is high for the person and October 1951 transfer. The exact day is explicitly supported by a recent archival working paper, not by a formal transfer decree recovered in this research.

## Evidence
1. **Exact day: Schiffman and Goldstein, 2026, printed p.62.** "October 8 [1951] The 3rd Government takes office, with Dov Yosef as Minister of Trade and Industry, with responsibility for food." Printed p.33 also says "Dov Yosef returned for a second term as food minister (October 1951-December 1952)". Footnote 9, p.6, says the official consumer publication Sherut Latzarchan was transferred to Trade and Industry "in October 1951, when Yosef replaced Lavon as food minister." Daniel Schiffman (Ariel University) and Eli Goldstein (Ashkelon Academic College), *Ambition Meets Reality: Pinhas Lavon's Animal-Protein Plan under Austerity and Rationing in Israel, 1950-1951*, March 2026, deposited June 2026. This is an academic working paper, not verified as peer-reviewed. I downloaded and visually checked the original PDF's p.6 and p.62.
https://mpra.ub.uni-muenchen.de/129488/
https://mpra.ub.uni-muenchen.de/129488/1/MPRA_paper_129488.pdf

2. **Primary corroboration: Knesset, 7 January 1952, questions 8-10.** Bar-Yehuda asks Yosef why consumer meetings still use party clubs after "שר-החקלאות לבון, אשר היה ממונה בזמנו על קיצוב צרכי מזון" (Agriculture Minister Lavon, who formerly had responsibility for rationing food necessities). Yosef replies as Trade and Industry Minister and points to his 31 December 1951 answer. An adjacent question concerns committees addressing distribution delays; Yosef describes them as working "בשיתוף פעולה עם משרדי" (in cooperation with my ministry). This directly establishes his food-rationing administration before summer 1952. The fetched text contains OCR/date glitches, so I do not rely on the question's parenthetical year for dating; the sitting header clearly dates the exchange to 7 January 1952. The scan itself could not be downloaded in this environment; this quote is checked against the official site's extracted text, not visually against the scan.
https://fs.knesset.gov.il/2/Plenum/2_ptm_250631.pdf
Alternate official copy: https://main.knesset.gov.il/News/PressReleases/Documents/110115b.pdf

3. **23 October 1951 speech and its contemporary reference.** Pinhas Yurman's Et-Mol article (May 1978), reproduced by Amal, dates Yosef's radio speech to 23 October 1951 and quotes his food-supply and anti-black-market policy. "כן תובטח עדיפות מטבע-חוץ למצרכי מזון חיוניים" (Priority in foreign currency will also be assured for essential foodstuffs). Schiffman/Goldstein p.33, footnote 121, independently cites the actual radio address as published in *Sherut Latzarchan* #13, 24 October 1951, archive file 708/2-Peh; footnote 120 cites Haaretz, 24 October 1951. I read the retelling and the paper, but did not personally recover those original newspaper/consumer-publication pages.
https://www.amalnet.k12.il/meida/history2/blog/2021/08/14/%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%A7-%D7%90%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA/

4. **Official cabinet record: 7 October 1951 presentation of the Third Government.** The roster lists "דב יוסף - מסחר ותעשייה ומשפטים" (Dov Yosef - Trade and Industry and Justice). This confirms his portfolio, but does not itself settle food responsibility or its transfer date. The preceding-day presentation is distinct from the 8 October government start.
https://main.knesset.gov.il/mk/government/documents/gov3.pdf

5. **Primary rationing practice, 28 November 1951.** During a soap-shortage debate Yosef answers as Trade and Industry Minister: "אין לנו מטבע חוץ במידה מספקת כדי לספק סבון בלי קיצוב" (We do not have enough foreign currency to supply soap without rationing). This supports rationing administration in late 1951, but soap alone cannot prove the food handover.
https://fs.knesset.gov.il/2/Plenum/2_ptm_250623.pdf

6. **Primary later continuity, 4 November 1952.** Yosef states his ministry works to maintain basic food rations "במנות שוות לכל נפש כעשיר כעני" (equal rations for every person, rich and poor). This confirms continuity later, not the start date.
https://fs.knesset.gov.il/%5C2%5CPlenum%5C2_ptm_250533.pdf

## The Rozin contradiction
Orit Rozin's article, printed p.93, really says: "נרשמה גם בקיץ 1952 כשתפקיד אספקת המזונות חזר לידי דב יוסף" (This was also recorded in summer 1952, when responsibility for food supply returned to Dov Yosef). The October 1951 speech, the 1952 Knesset questions and the later archival paper conflict with taking that as his first return date. I cannot establish why Rozin gives summer 1952. It could be an error or refer to a narrower change, but neither explanation is proved. Do not invent a separate transfer or use summer 1952 as the term's start. Prefer the explicitly dated evidence, and retain the discrepancy in the research note.
https://humanities.tau.ac.il/sites/humanities.tau.ac.il/files/media_server/humanities/zionism/%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C/1/Israel1_Rozin.pdf
