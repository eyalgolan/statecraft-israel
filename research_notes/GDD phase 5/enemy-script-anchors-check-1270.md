## 3. What the pages show that the note or the sheet does not

- **F1.** The Faluja pocket's Sudanese troops are 2,500 on O'Ballance p. 180, not 3,500. The note's Q-E8 and its C2 repeat the archive OCR's 3,500. - **F2.** The pages do not print 8 July as the day the first truce ended for these fronts. From the PDF text layer: Dupuy p. 73 prints "the four weeks of truce expired on July 8"; Dupuy p. 76 prints "By July 9, when the truce expired"; Dupuy p. 77 "started at nightfall on July 9, with the expiration of the truce"; Dupuy p. 80 "the cessation of the truce during the night of July 9-10"; O'Ballance p. 145 (page image) "On the resumption of hostilities, on July 9th" (and, in the archive's OCR text, his phase list: "From July 9th until July 18th").
