## 5. Conflicts between sources

1. **The 7th's position on the IDF map against the order of 23 May.** The map puts "שבע" near Hulda on 15.5.1948. The
   operation order (Ma'arachot, PDF p. 11) tells the brigade to concentrate at Na'an to Hulda by 05:00 on 23 May, and the
   diary of 15 May says the brigade "wasn't ready" and had got no men. The map is a later print (its "מג'דל (אשקלון)"
   label, `research_notes/Map borders/control-15-may-1948.md:87`) and draws a brigade founded on 14 to 16 May. It likely
   shows the brigades of the invasion's first weeks, not where the 7th stood on 15 May. Dupuy's "already deployed west of
   the Jerusalem Corridor" agrees with the map and not with the diary. **The agent reads the diary and the order as the
   stronger evidence; a person decides.**
2. **The 7th's founding date:** 14 May (the order, Ma'arachot; the row's `formed`), 15 May (he.wiki Bin Nun A, citing
   Naor's lexicon), 16 May (he.wiki 7th). `research_notes/M4 war mode/war-1948.md:78` has 16 May. The row follows the
   owner's ruling of 17 October 2026 ("14 May (the order)").
3. **Where the 7th first gathered:** a Tel Aviv hotel room (he.wiki 7th, the headquarters), Na'an (O'Ballance), the
   ground south of Givati (Dupuy). No source gives one place with a 15 May date.
4. **Yiftach's place on 15 May:** Safed (the IDF map), Camp Filon by Rosh Pinna (he.wiki Malkiya), and the
   battalions in the field at Malkiya and Qadas (he.wiki; Dupuy, "from nearby Ramat Naftali"). Safed and Rosh Pinna
   are a few kilometres apart; whether they fall on one hex was not checked (`tools/unit_anchor_hexes.py` would say).
   The IDF map is the only Israeli official source of the three.
5. **Yiftach's strength:** 4,500 "inc. some Golani" (en.wiki's undated brigade table; the row), about 2,000 (Dupuy),
   1,200 "in the north" (the diary of 15 May, if that is Yiftach). In units: 5, 2 or 1.
6. **Etzioni:** no conflict found. Every figure found is 3,166 and leads back to Ben-Gurion.
