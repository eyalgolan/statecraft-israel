# Phase 5 sourcing pass: prisoners, the war-loss total and the starting forces

Researched 2026-10-15. All URLs were checked on that date. Feeds GDD Phase 5 (#385): the prisoner return (impact plan section 4.3, Q-POW-3), the completion card's war-loss figure (Q-CIV-4), and the Arab starting forces for `content/map/units_1948.yaml` (the owner's decision on #570: "The Arab forces go in units_1948.yaml with `side` (including the ALA and the local forces) plus `strength` and `echelon`. The rows stay draft and Wikipedia-only until a sourcing pass against printed histories."). This note is that first pass, with free sources only.

This note is research, not a design. It adds no event text and changes no content file.

## Conventions

- **[V]** verified on a page opened on 2026-10-15, with the URL.
- **[S]** single source: a Wikipedia-only fact (tier 3) with its citation chain named, or a snippet.
- **[C]** sources conflict. Every version is listed.
- **[G]** not found; the searches tried are listed.
- One value per fact is given for the game rows. Conflicts are recorded next to it.
- Casualty, capture and strength figures are given only as a source gives them, with the version named. No quotation is reconstructed. Quoted wording below was read on the cited page.
- The prisoner and capture figures touch people who were held in wartime camps, and the war-loss figure counts the dead. Framing follows `docs/CONTENT_POLICY.md` section 1; any card text built from this note goes through D-F1 review.

## 1. Prisoner returns, per front (Q-POW-3)

The player's soldiers coming home, per front, with the counts both ways. Main source: the Jewish Virtual Library's "Prisoners-of-War and Captive Soldiers Exchanges" page [S, consolidated secondary], https://jewishvirtuallibrary.org/prisoners-of-war-and-captive-soldiers-exchanges, read 2026-10-15. The Israeli official source under CONTENT_POLICY section 1 is the MFA page "Background on Israeli POWs and MIAs" (26 January 2004; read via the Wayback capture of 22 November 2023, as the live page refuses scripts); JVL's text closely follows it, and where they differ both versions are [C] below. A note on the per-front "captured" totals: they mix soldiers and civilians (Jordan's 673, for example, includes the mostly-civilian Old City returnees, per he-Wikipedia citing the 1949 Hebrew press) - Q-POW-1 counts combatants only.

- **Egypt:** five Israelis held in the Faluja Pocket returned as the trapped Egyptian brigade was freed - **[C]** 26 February 1949 (MFA) vs 27 February 1949 (JVL); the Central Zionist Archives exhibition says January 1949. The main exchanges ran 7 to 9 March 1949 [S]; the ICRC archive inventory "C PALE" (entry C PALE-016.04, https://www.icrc.org/sites/default/files/external/doc/fr/assets/files/other/c_pale.pdf) lists three groups of 30, 59 and 55 Jewish prisoners exchanged on 7, 8 and 9 March, so **completion 9 March 1949**. The Jewish Telegraphic Agency bulletin of 11 March 1949 reports "the last party of Israeli prisoners, numbering 144", crossing with a celebration at Nitzanim [V]: https://www.jta.org/archive/israeli-lebanese-armistice-talks-continue-exchange-of-prisoners-of-war-virtually-set - recorded as the press version; 144 is the arithmetic total of the three ICRC groups, and a JTA bulletin usually carries the previous day's cable. **[C]** the MFA says "Feb 7-9, 1949" for the main exchange, almost certainly a slip for March (the armistice was signed 24 February). The start of the 21 days: the Egypt-Israel General Armistice Agreement, Article IX - "the exchange shall begin within ten days after the signing of this Agreement and shall be completed not later than twenty-one days following" (Avalon Project transcription, https://avalon.law.yale.edu/20th_century/arm01.asp, read 2026-10-15) - this closes the gap #605 lists for the Egypt card. Captured: Egypt 156 Israelis; Israel 1,098 Egyptians [S: JVL]. Across sources the figures range 133 to 156 Israelis and 1,098 to 1,145 Egyptians [C]: 133 from ICRC entry C PALE-013 (15 January 1949); 155 and 1,141 from the JTA bulletin of 8 March 1949; 1,145 from he-Wikipedia "שבויים ערבים במלחמת העצמאות", citing the 1949 Hebrew press. (The per-figure attributions are from this note's re-review; not re-fetched here.)
- **Jordan:** two groups of women taken at Gush Etzion's fall returned in June and November 1948; the POW exchanges ran 2 February to 3 March 1949 [S]. Captured: Jordan 673 Israelis; Israel 17 Jordanians [S].
- **Lebanon:** Article VI of the armistice (signed 23 March 1949) obliges exchange at Ras En Naqoura within 24 hours of the signing [V primary - the obligation, not the occurrence]: https://treaties.un.org/doc/Treaties/1949/10/19491006%2004-57%20PM/volume%2042-I-655-English_French.pdf (Israel-Lebanon General Armistice Agreement), read 2026-10-15. The date itself, 24 March 1949, rests on the MFA page and JVL [S]; a JTA bulletin of 25 March 1949 reports 36 Lebanese exchanged for seven Israelis [S, not re-fetched here]. Captured: Lebanon 8 Israelis; Israel 36 Lebanese [S].
- **Syria:** six Israelis returned 4 April 1949; the May returns **[C]** "between 2 and 8 May 1949" (JVL) vs a single date "May 2, 1949" with eight Israelis returned for 14 Syrians (MFA); the last exchange on 21 July 1949, the day after the armistice was signed [S]. The ICRC inventory (C PALE-016.01 and -016.02) matches 6 on 4 April and 31 on 21 July [S]. Context [V]: the JTA bulletin of 20 July 1949 says the mixed commission was to take up the prisoner-exchange problem: https://www.jta.org/archive/israeli-syrian-armistice-to-be-signed-today-mixed-commission-will-be-established. Captured: **[C]** Syria 48 Israelis and Israel 57 Syrians (JVL) vs 45 and 97 (MFA; the MFA's own rows sum to 45: 6 + 8 + 31).
- Sensitivity note: the JVL page's counts include "5,021 Palestinians and others" held by Israel. Any use goes through D-F1 review.
- **[G]** dated JTA reports of the Jordan and Syria exchanges: searched the JTA archive (1949 date-bounded), nothing found beyond the two bulletins above; the JVL page stands as their source.

## 2. The war-loss total (Q-CIV-4)

- **Total: 6,373 fallen out of a population of about 600,000** [V]. Source: the IDF History Department's journal "Yesodot", issue 3 (2022), Ofer Drori's article on the War of Independence decorations committees: "The War of Independence broke out on 30 November 1947 and ended on 17 July 1949; at the war's end 6,373 fallen were counted." https://www.idf.il/media/4zuhclym/yesodot_3_web_8_drorio.pdf, read 2026-10-15.
- **Breakdown:** 4,795 soldiers fell between 29 November 1947 and 20 July 1949, plus many civilians [V]. Source: Ministry of Defence archives, "The Price of War" exhibition: https://archives.mod.gov.il/sites/English/Exhibitions/David_Ben_Gurion_and_the_IDF/Pages/The-Price-of-War.aspx, read 2026-10-15. Civilians implied by the difference: about 1,578 (derived, not stated, **not for use on a card**). Published civilian figures are [C]: "more than 2,000 civilians" (the IDF English page below); about 2,000 as an estimate (Y. Harkabi, Maarachot 166, March 1965, citing the IDF History Branch); 931 in the National Insurance Institute's narrower "victims of hostile acts" category.
- **Framing:** the Knesset's backgrounder gives "more than 6,000 dead, almost 1% of the total population, more than 4,000 soldiers" [V], consistent: https://m.knesset.gov.il/About/Occasion/Documents/IndependenceWar.pdf, read 2026-10-15.
- **Second Israeli official source for 6,373:** the MFA page "Israel's War of Independence (1947 - 1949)" says "It cost 6,373 killed in action (from pre-state days until 20 July 1949) almost 1% of the yishuv" [V via the Wayback capture of 11 December 2011]: https://web.archive.org/web/20111211101529/http://www.mfa.gov.il/MFA/History/Modern+History/Israel+wars/Israels+War+of+Independence+-+1947+-+1949.htm, read 2026-10-15. It ties the count to 20 July, which bears on the end-date [C] below.
- **The owner's ruling (15 October 2026): Q-CIV-4 = "A: Israeli total only"** (https://github.com/eyalgolan/statecraft-israel/issues/375#issuecomment-6183762894). The ruled card wording is "More than 6,000 Israelis were killed in the war, about one in a hundred of the Jewish population; more than 4,000 of them were soldiers" - the Knesset round figures, with 6,373 as the precise backing value. The card says "killed" (the ruling's word); this note keeps "fallen" as the memorial term.
- **[C]** war end-date: the IDF article says 17 July 1949; the MOD counts to 20 July 1949 (the Syrian signing day).
- **[C]** the IDF's English wars page says the loss "amounted to more than 10% of the young country's population" - arithmetically inconsistent with 6,373 of about 600,000 (about 1%, as the Knesset says); treated as an error on the English page: https://www.idf.il/en/mini-sites/wars-and-operations/israels-war-of-independence-1948/ (the live page blocks scripted fetches; confirmed on the Wayback capture of 16 July 2026), read 2026-10-15. The same page gives "some 4,000 of its soldiers and more than 2,000 civilians".
- **Window note:** the 6,373 covers the full war from 30 November 1947, while the MVP runs from 15 May 1948. A split for the MVP window was not found broken down by period; if the card wants the window only, that is a follow-up.

## 3. The starting forces, both sides, 15 May 1948 (Q-START-4 pick: armies + ALA + local forces)

**The repo already holds the pass this section set out to do.** `research_notes/GDD phase 5/arab-forces-1948.md` (merged by #419, refs #226) covers both dates (15 May and 11 June 1948), per army, from printed histories read in full (O'Ballance, Dupuy, al-Tall, Qawuqji's memoirs, the CIA weekly summaries and FRUS), with positions, strengths, conflicts and gaps. It supersedes a Wikipedia-level pass, and Phase 5 PR 1 should write its rows from it.

Two cross-checks this pass adds:

- Elhanan Oren's survey of the war (the author was a researcher in the IDF History Department; published in Alon Kadish, ed., "The War of Independence 1948-1949: A Renewed Discussion" (מלחמת העצמאות תש"ח-תש"ט: דיון מחודש), the Society for the Study of the Israel Defense Forces and the Ministry of Defense Publishing House, 2004; scan on the IDF site, https://www.idf.il/media/is5dlct2/%D7%A1%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%AA_%D7%9E%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A8_%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%97%D7%A0%D7%9F_%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%92%D7%9E%D7%A8.pdf, read 2026-10-15): the invading armies totalled only about 30,000 at the invasion; the Haganah had about nine brigades by independence; its mobilized strength grew from about 20,000 (early April) to 36,500 (mid-May) [V]. The repo note's Glubb/Shlaim/Rogan totals (21,500 to under 25,000 on 15 May, regulars) sit below Oren's 30,000, but Oren counts expeditionary combat troops and the note's sources count field armies in Palestine - both are recorded, neither preferred [C].
- The Israeli 15 May figure: 29,677 (the Wikipedia infobox [S], whose single footnote is Gelber (2006), p. 12, which says the count includes logistical as well as combat personnel; the infobox does not date it to 15 May, and no Morris (2004) reference was found on that value) against Oren's 36,500 for mid-May [V] - [C], both recorded.
