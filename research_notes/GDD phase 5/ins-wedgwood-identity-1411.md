## What the sources say

The hull is HMCS *Beauharnois* (K540), a modified Flower-class corvette. The Aliyah Bet ship was called *Wedgwood* in 1946. The question is the name and number she carried in the Israeli Navy in 1948.

## What this settles and what it does not

- **K 18 is the pennant, not K 20.** Palyam, Machal, he.wiki, the en.wiki infobox and list all give K 18 for this hull; K 20 for *Haganah* (ex-*Norsyd*) is given by the Machal "Illegal Ships" page, he.wiki and the en.wiki *Norsyd* infobox and list (Palyam gives no K 20). The en.wiki *Beauharnois* body text ("Hashomer, designated the K 20") disagrees with its own infobox and with every other source read that gives a pennant number, so it is **[C]**, with the weight on K 18. This is the mismatch the row's `to_verify` item describes.
- **The Israeli name is Hashomer in the two non-Wikipedia English sources and he.wiki; "Wedgwood" is in use alongside it [C].** Palyam and Machal call the 1948 navy ship *Ha'Shomer*, and call *Wedgwood* the 1946 immigrant ship. he.wiki's page is titled "אח"י וג'ווד" (infobox heading "אח"י וג'ווד (ק-18)") and it says Wedgwood is a nickname of the corvette *Hashomer*; the Palmach page and, per the row's own comment (not re-read, see below), the University of Haifa study call the navy ship "אח"י ווג'ווד". Which was the official name, and whether both were in use in 1948, is not shown by anything read. he.wiki cites *Ma'arachot Yam* 64 (September 1963) and *Shoval* (August 1998), neither opened [GAP].
- **Smaller conflicts, recorded:** commissioning date 9 June 1948 (en.wiki list, For Posterity's Sake, he.wiki) vs 9 September 1948 (en.wiki infobox); Palyam calls her the "second vessel to be commissioned"; Machal's sentence "the Israel Navy's first , the former 'Wedgwood,'" is missing a noun, and the same page says *Northland* sailed first, on 21 May 1948, so no firm conflict is recorded.
