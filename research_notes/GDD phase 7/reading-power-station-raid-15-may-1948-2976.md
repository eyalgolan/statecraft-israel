# Did the 15 May 1948 dawn raid on Tel Aviv hit the Reading power station? (research note, 2026-11-11)

Status: **AI draft, not checked by a person.** Research only. Nothing here changes `content/`, `sim/` or `unity/`. Refs #2976. An AI agent read the sources and wrote this note. It lists pages and printed text for a person to check. It is not that check.

## Question

#2976 follows PR #2968, the Tel Aviv raid card on New game (`unity/Assets/Resources/RaidIntro/raid-intro.yaml`). On the card's power station question the owner answered "Add after a check" (PR #2968 body, "Questions for the owner", item 2: https://github.com/eyalgolan/statecraft-israel/pull/2968). The issue asks for a source showing that the dawn raid on 15 May 1948 struck the Reading power station or the area around it, and for a person to read that source.

## Short answer

- **Supported, for the dawn raid, with the damage slight.** Ben-Gurion's diary for 15 May 1948 dates the raid to his radio broadcast at about 05:15. It says the Reading station "was hit as well" (rows B1, B2).
- Nicolle et al. (2024) cite an Israeli account of the same raid: at around 0500 a Spitfire "dived on Reading Power Station" and its two bombs caused "slight damage to the power station" (row N1). he.wiki says the Egyptian attacks did the station no significant damage, but gives no date and no footnote (row W2). Hit, with slight damage, fits all three.
- **Not found:** a 15 May press report naming the station. JTA (row J1) and the Egyptian communiques quoted by Nicolle do not name it. Davar, Haaretz and the Palestine Post could not be reached (search log).
- **Not read:** Morris, *1948* (2008), p. 261, which en.wiki cites for the same claim (row W1).

## Conventions

- Every source below was opened on **2026-11-11**.
- Tags (this lane's brief): **[V]** a page image was seen; **[S]** secondary, or primary text read only as a web transcription; **[C]** sources disagree, every value kept; **[GAP]** nothing reachable settles it.
- Quotation marks are verbatim from what was read. Spelling is as printed.
- 15 May 1948 was a Saturday. "Dawn raid" means the first raid, at about 05:00 to 05:15. Later raids on 15 May are kept apart.
