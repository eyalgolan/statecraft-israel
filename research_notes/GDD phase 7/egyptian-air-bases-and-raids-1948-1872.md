# Where the Egyptian Spitfires and Dakotas flew from in 1948, and what they raided (research note, 2026-10-28)

Status: **AI draft, not checked by a person.** Research only. Nothing here changes `content/`, `sim/` or `unity/`. Refs #1872. The note was read and written by an AI agent for a contributor. It is a reading list with pages and printed text for a person to check, not the check itself. Placement of the Egyptian Spitfire and Dakota C-47 models follows in a separate PR.

## Question

Tal Heuberger's Egyptian Spitfire and Dakota C-47 models need map positions and dates. Which airfields did the Royal Egyptian Air Force (REAF) fly them from in 1948 (for example el-Arish, al-Majdal), from what dates, and which raid targets in Israel are sourced with a day?

## Short answer (what the sources read support)

- **el-Arish (Sinai) is the one Egyptian airfield every source read agrees on.** Nicolle et al. call it the Tactical Air Force's "only 'front-line' aerodrome" in May 1948; JTA shows Israel bombing it on 16 July (bulletin image) and, in a web page, in October. See rows A1 to A4.
- **al-Majdal is not documented as an airfield in anything read.** It appears as a Muslim Brotherhood volunteer HQ before the war and as a town the Egyptian Army entered on 21 May 1948. It stays [GAP] as an air base (row A6).
- **Gaza airfield** (the old RAF field): Israel bombed it as "unused" on the night of 20-21 May (Nicolle); a Jewish Agency spokesman said the Egyptians "now occupy it" (JTA, item datelined 21 May). Both are recorded, neither shows REAF aircraft based there (row A5).
- **Dakotas:** the REAF's C-47s flew supplies Almaza (Cairo) to el-Arish and were used as improvised bombers. The start date is **[C]** inside Nicolle et al.: p. 23 counts "four bombing missions ... flown by Dakotas" on 15 May, while p. 34 calls 18 May the "first recorded use of two C-47 'Dakota bombers'" (rows D3, D4). The take-off base is also **[C]**: Table 1 (p. 10) puts a Bomber Flight with 5 modified Dakotas in No. 3 Squadron at Almaza, while p. 23 lists the Dakota bombing missions among those flown from al-Arish (row D3).
- **Sourced raid targets with a day:** Sde Dov on 15 May and again on 17 May (Nicolle pp. 23, 32); Tel Aviv harbour, Kfar Sirkin and Negba on 17 May (Nicolle p. 32); the Old Tel Aviv central bus station on 18 May; Tel Aviv airport on 19 May (Egyptian Communique No. 7 in Nicolle p. 37), and Tel Aviv and Negba on 19 May (JTA 20 May, dateline 19 May, with Nicolle p. 37); Ramat David (RAF) on 22 May. Sde Dov after 22 May: one more attack, with no day in any source read (row R5, [GAP]).
