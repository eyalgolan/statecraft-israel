# Event-card photographs: the four remaining portrait-fallback events

Searched 16 October 2026 (fork research lane), continuing `armistice-card-images-1949.md`. Licence ruling
(`docs/event-images.md`, owner ruling of 11 October 2026): public domain or CC0, plus archival event photographs
(not portraits) under CC BY 4.0 with a credit line. GPO, PD-Israel-only, PD-India-only and share-alike refused.

## education_registration_1951 (Schools in the Transit Camps) - located, but rights unconfirmed [G]

"At the Shobaki Mabara kindergarten", Herzliya - on subject and window (the page's date field says 1951;
"January" comes only from the original file name), but not yet a qualifying candidate. The file page's Author is
{{unknown}}; Israel Preker is named as מעלה הצילום, the person who uploaded the photo to PikiWiki, not the
photographer; the source is the Beit Rishonim archive (Herzliya history museum). So the {{PD-author|Israel
Preker}} dedication is the uploader's, and "released into the public domain worldwide by the photographer" is not
supported. [G] until Beit Rishonim confirms the rights.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:122188_herzliya_-_mabara_PikiWiki_Israel.png, checked 2026-10-16.

## rhodes_talks (Talks at Rhodes) - no candidate; Jordan-round frames kept as context only [C]

The card is the January 1949 decision to send a delegation to the Rhodes talks with **Egypt**
(`content/events/rhodes_talks.yaml`, 9-13 January 1949). The two Benno Rothenberg frames located
(CC BY 4.0, NLI / Meitar / Pritzker, VRT-confirmed) - "Moshe Dayan and Walter Eytan", 997008137544105171 and
997008137543805171 - do not show those talks: both file pages title them "משלחת מטעם ממשלת ישראל בדרך לשיחות עם
נציגי הממלכה הירדנית" (the Israeli government delegation on its way to talks with the Jordanian kingdom's
representatives), dated February 1949
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Moshe_Dayan_and_Walter_Eytan_(997008137544105171).jpg and
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Moshe_Dayan_and_Walter_Eytan_(997008137543805171).jpg, checked
2026-10-16) [V]. That is the Jordan round a month later, not the Egypt talks this card is about [C]; a third
frame from the same shoot already serves `armistice_jordan`. The two frames are kept here as context, not as
candidates; whether Jordan-round frames may illustrate this card anyway is the owner's call. No photograph of
the January Egypt talks with a qualifying licence was found (the known table shots are PD-Israel or UN Photo,
both refused): [G] for the card itself.

## forex_crisis_1951 (Foreign Currency Runs Out) - candidates from the austerity regime

"Shopkeepers' strike after the government's decision on clothing rationing", Tel Aviv, August 1950 - two
Rothenberg frames, CC BY 4.0: 997008137472105171 and 997008137472305171
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tel_Aviv-Yafo_997008137472105171.jpg and
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tel_Aviv-Yafo_997008137472305171.jpg, checked 2026-10-16). Also
qualifying: a Purim costume of a girl dressed half "white market", half "black market" (24 March 1951, CC BY 4.0,
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tel_Aviv-Yafo_997008137208105171.jpg) and a shoe-shop window sign
praising the austerity regime (Allenby Street, December 1949, CC BY 4.0,
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tel_Aviv-Yafo_997008137389205171.jpg). None is from the crisis month
itself (October 1951); all are the regime the crisis belongs to. [V] as context photographs; [G] for the
crisis month itself.
