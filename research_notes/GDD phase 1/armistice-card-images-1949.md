# Armistice-card photographs: Egypt, Lebanon, Jordan

Searched 16 October 2026 (fork research lane). Licence ruling (`docs/event-images.md`, owner ruling of 11 October
2026): public domain or CC0, plus one narrow exception - archival event photographs (not portraits) under CC BY 4.0
with a credit line. GPO photographs, files whose only licence is PD-Israel or PD-India, and share-alike files are
refused. Since this note was written, the portrait rule widened (#853, merged 17 October 2026): "Portraits may
also be CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-SA 4.0 photographs, on the terms of principle 5 in section 1 (owner ruling, 17 October
2026: "Allow CC BY / BY-SA"): each is credited in `NOTICE`, and a portrait cropped from a CC BY-SA 4.0 photograph
is itself released under CC BY-SA 4.0" (`docs/CONTENT_POLICY.md` section 4). `docs/event-images.md` adds: "The
rules for event photographs below are unchanged" - so every refusal in this note stands.

## Egypt - already covered; a real signing photograph exists but is refused

`armistice_egypt` already carries Benno Rothenberg's Faluja-pocket context photograph (CC BY 4.0, NLI / Meitar /
Pritzker; `unity/Assets/UI/EventImages/event-images.yaml`).

A real signing photograph exists on Wikimedia Commons: "Dayan Rhodos.jpg" - Moshe Dayan signing the Israel-Egypt
General Armistice Agreement, Rhodes, 24 February 1949
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dayan_Rhodos.jpg, checked 2026-10-16). Its only tag is PD-Israel (author
unknown; the page itself notes no US public-domain tag), a refused class. [G] for a qualifying signing photograph.

## Jordan - already covered; no signing photograph found

`armistice_jordan` already carries Rothenberg's delegation-beside-a-UN-aircraft photograph (CC BY 4.0). Two sibling
frames from the same shoot are on Commons under the same CC BY 4.0 licence, if a closer view is ever wanted:
"Moshe Dayan and Walter Eytan"
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Moshe_Dayan_and_Walter_Eytan_(997008137544105171).jpg and
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Moshe_Dayan_and_Walter_Eytan_(997008137543805171).jpg, checked 2026-10-16;
NLI caption: the Israeli delegation on its way to talks with Jordan's representatives; catalogue date "February
1949" is loose - the Jordan agreement was signed 3 April 1949). Credit line: Benno Rothenberg / Meitar Collection /
National Library of Israel / The Pritzker Family National Photography Collection · CC BY 4.0; VRTS ticket
2021092910005788. Both are near-portraits of named officials (Dayan and Eytan), so if they are ever used they pass
under the 17 October portrait rule and need a `NOTICE` credit. No signing photograph found. [G] for a signing
photograph.

## Lebanon - the one open gap: no qualifying photograph

`armistice_lebanon` still falls back to the minister's portrait. The one venue photograph found:
"Riley makleff.jpg" - US Gen. Riley and Lt. Col. M. Makleff at Ras el-Nakura, by Hugo Mendelson, National Photo
Collection / Government Press Office, digital ID D768-024 (other versions D160-037, D768-021)
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Riley_makleff.jpg, checked 2026-10-16). GPO photographs are a refused
class. No signing photograph of the 23 March 1949 Lebanon agreement found in any archive searched. [G]
