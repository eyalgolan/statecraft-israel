## 1. The Tulkarm bypass, 1949 (Q-RAIL-1, Task 2.0)

The corridors note records: "The bypass's own alignment west of Tulkarm is not given by any source read [U]." What a dated press pass adds:

- **[V] The bypass was completed by 4 August 1949.** Haaretz, 4 August 1949, headline read on the scan: "Train services to Haifa in September - the railway section bypassing Tulkarm completed" (הושלם קטע המסילה העוקף את טול כרם); trains were to run Tel Aviv-Haifa via Petah Tikva-Rosh HaAyin from mid-September. https://www.nli.org.il/he/newspapers/haretz/1949/08/04/01/article/12, checked 2026-10-15.
- **[V] The line opened 20 September 1949.** Ma'ariv, 20 September 1949, page one, headline read on the scan: "The railway line along the Shfela was opened" (נפתח קו הרכבת לאורך השפלה); the OCR transcript has the first train Haifa-Tel Aviv running that morning with a ceremony. https://www.nli.org.il/he/newspapers/mar/1949/09/20/01/article/1, checked 2026-10-15.
- **[S] The alignment in words:** the new track ran right along the Green Line south of Hadera (התוואי החדש, מדרום לחדרה, עבר ממש על הקו הירוק); a section of about 2 km near Tulkarm station stayed in Jordanian hands. HE Wikipedia "רכבת ישראל" and "תחנת הרכבת טולכרם", citing the same dated press, checked 2026-10-15. A surveyed alignment is in no source read; how fine the corridor's trace may be stays with the owner.
- **[C]** EN Wikipedia "Palestine Railways" says Israel "bypassed Tulkarm with a short stretch of new track" in August 1948 (a war-time track?); the dated press records completion 4 August 1949 and service from 20 September 1949. Recorded, not resolved.
- **Still [U]:** the 7 October 1948 Latrun-road bus agreement (corridors note) - not checked; no plan row waits on it.

## 3. Camp-site candidates (Task 1.0, Q-CAMP-2)

Candidates, each with its place and date sourced; the plan's rule applies: "If a candidate has no source for its place and its date, it is not a site."

- **Tzrifin (Sarafand), near Rishon LeZion - the strongest candidate.** The British left 15 May 1948; Givati captured the camp 18-19 May 1948 [S: HE Wikipedia "צריפין"; the state's memorial page at https://www.izkor.gov.il/monument/en_ce95202c4ac303d9789b983d629a887b; both checked 2026-10-15]. It became an IDF camp at once, and Bahad 4, the national basic-training base, sat there from 1949 [S: HE Wikipedia "בה"ד 4", checked 2026-10-15]. A prisoner-of-war camp on the site held Arabs during the war (over 1,100 in December 1948; closed summer 1949) - any card text built from this goes through D-F1 review.
- **Schneller camp, Jerusalem.** The British evacuated 17 March 1948 and handed the camp to Etzioni ("permanent camp no. 1"); it was the Jerusalem district headquarters, and Harel's fifth battalion based there for operations in the city, 1948 [S: HE Wikipedia "בית היתומים שנלר"; palmach.org.il archive caption, dated 1948; both checked 2026-10-15]. Later sources describe it as Etzioni's training camp.
- **Julis camp, near Gedera - weaker anchor.** Golani camped there from December 1948 (before Operations Assaf and Horev) - an operational presence, not a training-base claim; the row is Wikipedia-only in `content/map/units_1948.yaml` (checked 2026-10-11 by that PR).
- **Ein Shemer, near Hadera - weaker anchor.** The 7th Brigade "organised for a time" there in June 1948 - operational, Wikipedia-only (same file, same check date).

## 4. The Hula swamp site against Task 5.0 criterion 4

Recorded factually for the owner, per the criterion's exception clause: the Hula marsh's papyrus economy was worked by the Ghawarna villages. Al-Salihiyya, in the marsh area four km north of the lake, was depopulated on 25 May 1948 in Operation Yiftach [S: PalQuest, https://www.palquest.org/en/place/16877/al-salihiyya; Zochrot, https://www.zochrot.org/villages/village_details/49353/en; EN Wikipedia; all checked 2026-10-15], as were neighbouring marsh villages (al-Dawwara, al-Muftakhira, others). The lake bed itself was concession land, but the swamp site's surroundings include depopulated villages whose lands met the marsh. Whether the site's hex clears criterion 4 is the owner's call.
