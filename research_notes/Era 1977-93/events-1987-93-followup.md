## A1. First days of the first intifada, 9 to 11 December 1987

- **9 December 1987.** JTA, bulletin 10 December 1987 (no. 231), page 2, "Arab Student Killed in Gaza Unrest",
  https://www.jta.org/archive/arab-student-killed-in-gaza-unrest. Dateline "TEL AVIV, Dec. 9 (JTA)". Printed text:
  "Israel Defense Force soldiers killed a 17-year-old Arab high school student and wounded at least 10 others, one
  seriously, after their vehicle came under a hail of fire-bombs at the Jebaliya refugee camp in the northern Gaza
  Strip Wednesday", and "A curfew was imposed on the refugee camp." Wednesday is 9 December 1987 (computed here). [V, agent read 25 Oct 2026; a person's check pending]
- **10 December 1987.** JTA, bulletin 11 December 1987 (no. 232), page 4, "Unrest Mounts in Territories as Arab Is
  Killed in Nablus", dateline "JERUSALEM, Dec. 10 (JTA)" (OCR only for the quoted lines). It says "Violence spread in the
  Gaza Strip Thursday in the aftermath of the fatal shooting of a 17-year-old high school student and the wounding of
  10 others by IDF soldiers Wednesday." [V, agent read 25 Oct 2026; a person's check pending]
- **[C] with the first note's A1 on the numbers.** The first note records both Wikipedia pages giving 16 wounded and
  differing on the age band of the person killed. JTA, written the same day, gives a 17-year-old and "at least 10"
  wounded. All three versions stand: he.wiki and en.wiki (first note, [S]) and JTA ([V, agent read 25 Oct 2026; a person's check pending]).
- **What these bulletins do not cover.** No item in the bulletin of 9 December 1987 (no. 230) reports the collision of
  8 December. Its OCR text was searched for "truck", "lorry", "Jebaliya", "Jabalya", "riot" and "unrest" and has no
  such item (searched, not read in full). The 8 December trigger therefore still rests on the two Wikipedia pages in the
  first note. [S] Bulletin 14 December 1987 (no. 233) has a 13 December item in which the defence minister is quoted
  blaming "the recent wave of unrest in the administered territories" on outside states (OCR only).

## A2. Mothers' Bus attack, 7 March 1988

JTA, bulletin 8 March 1988 (no. 45), page 1, "Three Israelis Killed in Negev During Terrorist Hijack and Rampage",
https://www.jta.org/archive/three-israelis-killed-in-negev-during-terrorist-hijack-and-rampage. Dateline
"TEL AVIV, March 7 (JTA)". [V, agent read 25 Oct 2026; a person's check pending]
- **Date and times.** "Monday morning in a three-and-a-half-hour terrorist bus hijack and shooting rampage in the
  Negev"; the shooting began "at about 7 a.m. local time"; the assault on the bus "was carried out at 10:25 a.m. local
  time and lasted less than a minute". Monday is 7 March 1988 (computed here). This agrees with the date in the first
  note and gives the clock times.
- **[C] on casualties.** JTA: "Three Israeli civilians were killed and 10 wounded", of whom "nine of the wounded were bus
  passengers; the tenth was a man traveling on the road at the time of the hijacking". The first note, from both
  Wikipedia pages: three killed and eight wounded. Both stand.
- **[C] on the sequence (a third version).** JTA: "Two women and a man, all passengers in the bus, were fatally shot.
  The man was killed by the terrorists while senior Israel Defense Force officers were bargaining with them to gain time.
  The women were killed by bullets later, as border police stormed the bus." That matches the first note's he.wiki body
  (one before, two during) and not en.wiki's lead.
- **Number aboard.** "The bus was packed, but most of the 50 passengers managed to escape unhurt."
- **Who the attackers were.** The same item gives two versions: one passenger's account ("an Arabic speaking woman in the bus who conversed with them") that they said they were from
  one movement, and the defence minister's statement to the Knesset naming another. Not resolved here. [C]
- The first note's [GAP] on a JTA bulletin for this attack is closed for the date, times and casualties above.

## A15. The day Rabin signed the letters, 9 or 10 September 1993

  Sunday-dated item puts Friday at 10 September 1993 (computed here). [V, agent read 25 Oct 2026; a person's check pending]
- **Expected Friday morning.** JTA, bulletin 10 September 1993 (no. 171), page 1, dateline "JERUSALEM, Sept. 9 (JTA)" (https://www.jta.org/archive/israel-agrees-to-recognize-plo-in-a-historic-reversal-of-policy):

  morning". The bulletin's own printed text of the two letters (page 2, OCR only) is dated "Sept. 9". [V, agent read 25 Oct 2026; a person's check pending]
- **The letter's own date.** United Nations Information System on the Question of Palestine, "Israel-PLO recognition
  - Exchange of letters between PM Rabin and Chairman Arafat", https://www.un.org/unispal/?p=205528 (opened
  25 October 2026), a reproduction filed as "Non-UN documents": the Rabin letter is headed "September 9, 1993" and
