# CBS Israeli departures, 1990-1993: source follow-up

Researched 3 November 2026. Research only, Refs #147: no content rows, code, calibration targets or goldens. This supplies a partial source for section 3a of [the population note](population-1977-93.md); a person's check remains pending.

**Release and definition.** Printed release title: `Departures and Returns in 2020 of Israelis Staying Abroad Continuously for a Year or More`; printed chart title: `תרשים 2 - ישראלים יוצאים, מספרים מוחלטים ושיעורים ל 1,000 תושבים באוכלוסייה, 1990 - 2020`. CBS media release 281/2022 is dated 23 August 2022; its first page marks corrections to pages 1-2 on 16 February 2023. The release defines Israeli departures as Israelis who stayed abroad for 365 consecutive days or more and had stayed in Israel for at least 90 consecutive days before leaving (page 13, also footnote 4 on page 3). Source: https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/mediarelease/doclib/2022/281/01_22_281b.pdf . Pages 1, 3 and 13 were read on the page images. [V, agent read 3 November 2026; a person's check pending]
