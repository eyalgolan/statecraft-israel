# Procurement and the defence industry, 1977 to 1993

Researched 24 October 2026. Every URL below was opened on that date by an agent. **Research only:** a note for issue #157
(1977-1993 item 13, part of #51), the research part the owner opened on 24 Oct. It adds no event text, no project
definition and no number for a game file; nothing under `content/` is changed. The procurement content stays paused until
after the MVP. This is a new note, with no earlier version on main.

## Purpose

For each procurement project the issue names: its dates, what each side said, and the source. The projects are the Lavi
from 1980 to the cabinet vote of August 1987, the Merkava (Mark 1 to Mark 3), the US aircraft (F-15 and F-16) with their
delivery years, the US aid figures that paid for them, and Dimona (design §10.5 and 1957-77 D8: the public record only).
The design is [the 1977-1993 design](../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-era-1977-1993-design.md) (§2.5 row on the Lavi
and the to-verify line; §10.5; §12 item 13). The structure follows the 1957-77 note `research_notes/Era 1957-77/procurement-1957-77.md`
(owner-reviewed in PR #1607; not linked here because it is not on main yet).

## Conventions

The same as the other notes in this folder and the 1957-77 procurement note:

- **[V]** read this session on a primary source opened: FRUS, a Statute at Large, official gazettes or decrees, Knesset or
  government records. Press is never [V], even when the original bulletin page was read. Every [V] here is an agent read and
  is written in full each time as `[V, agent read 2026-10-24; a person's check pending]` (CONTENT_POLICY §2 item 6). A US
  document is [V, agent read 2026-10-24; a person's check pending] for what the US government wrote or was told, not for the
  fact behind it. A FRUS editorial footnote is the editors' reading of a document they cite; where the cited document was
  not opened, the row says so. A newspaper or JTA report is [S] for what was reported on that day, not for the fact reported.
- **[S]** single or secondary source: press (JTA bulletins, newspapers as JTA relayed them, statements as the press reported
  them, even where the bulletin page was read), agreement of several secondary sources, Wikipedia read at the revision
  (oldid) in the source list, or a database.
- **[C]** sources conflict; every value is kept.
- **[GAP]** not found in a source that could be opened. Every [GAP] says what was read or searched.
- No figure, name, page or quote was filled from memory. Quotes are copied from the page; JTA's curly quotation marks are
  written straight. A search snippet or summary is never a source: a detail stays [GAP] until the record itself was opened.
- **JTA dates.** The date on a JTA archive page is the bulletin date. The event date is read from the dateline on the item
  in the original bulletin PDF (the "See Original Daily Bulletin" link), and "today", "yesterday" and weekdays are
  resolved from that dateline. The PDFs were read by OCR, so a dateline can be garbled; where one could not be read, the row
  says so, gives the bulletin date and resolves a weekday from the bulletin's own weekday only.
- **Negative searches** are in the search log (section 9) with the exact query, URL pattern, date range, the count returned
  and the day run.
- **Sensitive content (D8).** Nothing here states or implies a capability. Press items about Dimona that state or allege
  one are not used; only what a government said in public is (section 6, which goes to the owner as sensitive content).

## Source caveat

- **FRUS 1977-80** (Office of the Historian, TEI files at https://github.com/HistoryAtState/frus, `volumes/<id>.xml`, the source of
  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/<volume>/d<n>): volumes 8 (Arab-Israeli Dispute, January 1977 to August 1978)
  and 9, second edition (August 1978 to December 1980). Citations give volume and document number.
- **FRUS 1981-88 was not read.** The TEI files for volumes XVIII part 1, XIX and XXII in that repository hold only the
  title page (2.5 KB each) and `https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1981-88v19/d1` answered HTTP 404 on 24 Oct, so no
  FRUS document from 1981 on is used. Everything from 1981 to 1993 rests on a Statute at Large, JTA and Wikipedia.
- **Statutes at Large:** Public Law 99-83 (99 Stat. 190, 8 August 1985) from govinfo.gov, text extracted with Ghostscript.
- **JTA archive** (https://www.jta.org/archive) through its own search API, with the original bulletin PDFs
  (pdfs.jta.org) for datelines.
- **CRS** "U.S. Foreign Aid to Israel" (RL33222, version of 28 May 2025, read through the mirror
  https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/RL33222.html): opened, and it has no year-by-year series for 1977-1993, so no
  figure from it is used. The congress.gov and gao.gov hosts answered HTTP 403.
- **Israeli tier 1 sources** (Knesset records, Ministry of Defense, IDF Archives, gov.il, CBS) were not retried; the
  other notes in this folder record them as blocked. Nothing here rests on them.
- **Not read:** SIPRI data, the US Department of State Bulletin, the New York Times, Haaretz and Maariv (read only as JTA
  reported them), GAO and Congressional hearing reports.
