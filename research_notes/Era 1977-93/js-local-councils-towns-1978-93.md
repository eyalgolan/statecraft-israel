# Judea and Samaria local councils and towns founded 1978 to 1993 (research note, 2026-10-25)

Status: research only. Nothing here is in `content/`, `sim/`, `tools/` or `unity/`; the dated settlements file stays paused
until after the MVP. Refs #165 (1977-93 21), the settlements part of the 1977-1993
design in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-era-1977-1993-design.md`, sections 7.3 and 7.4. One topic of #165 only: the
Judea and Samaria rows of the CBS localities file 2024 with a founding year of 1978 to 1993 whose local authority is the
locality's own local council or its own municipality (not a regional council). **Every Judea and Samaria row of the CBS 2024 file with a founding year of 1978-1993 whose locality is its own local council or town is in this note. The other 68 rows of 1978-1993 are regional-council rows: the Arvot HaYarden and Megilot rows are in `research_notes/Era 1977-93/jordan-valley-localities-1977-93.md` (#1665), the Mateh Binyamin rows in `research_notes/Era 1977-93/binyamin-localities-1978-93.md` (#1669), the Shomron rows in `research_notes/Era 1977-93/shomron-localities-1978-93.md` (#1671) and the Gush Etzion and Har Hevron rows in `research_notes/Era 1977-93/gush-etzion-har-hevron-localities-1978-93.md` (#1670). A review agent's check (agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending) matched all 78 rows against these five notes and found each in exactly one (see "The 68 rows"). East Jerusalem neighbourhoods are not covered.** Gaza, Sinai, the Golan, the rows of 1967-77 and the lines are not in this note. Which rows enter the game, and their names, wait for the owner's sensitivity list (#118); this note makes no include or omit choice, no naming choice and no statement on the status of any place beyond what a source says, quoted and tagged.

It follows the format of `research_notes/GDD objectives/settlements-1949-51.md` and of the sibling notes `research_notes/Era 1977-93/jordan-valley-localities-1977-93.md` (#1665) and `research_notes/Era 1957-77/jordan-valley-localities-1967-77.md`. Every page below was opened on **25 October 2026** (UTC) unless a row says otherwise.

The question: which Israeli localities of Judea and Samaria that are today their own local council or town were founded between 1978 and 1993, and for each: the founding date (and what it marks), coordinates from a named source, and the sources.

## Short answer

- **The list.** The Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS) localities file 2024, rows whose district is "אזור יהודה ושומרון" (Judea and Samaria, the file's own district name), with a year of founding of 1978 to 1993 and a local authority that is not a regional council (the file's local-authority type code is 1 "עירייה" (municipality) or 2 "מועצה מקומית" (local council)): **10 rows** (2 of type 1, 8 of type 2). The district has 154 rows, 78 of them with a year of 1978 to 1993; the other 68 rows are regional-council rows (Shomron 20, Mateh Binyamin 19, Har Hevron 12, Gush Etzion 7, Arvot HaYarden 7, Megilot 3). By CBS year: 1978 (2), 1980 (1), 1981 (1), 1983 (3), 1985 (2), 1986 (1). **No row has a year of 1979, 1982 or 1984 or of 1987 to 1993.** The ten: Ariel, Qarne Shomron, Efrat, Bet Arye-Ofarim, Alfe Menashe, Immanu'el, Giv'at Ze'ev, Betar Illit, Oranit and Har Adar.
- **Which of the 68 rows this note takes.** The sibling note of #1665 (`research_notes/Era 1977-93/jordan-valley-localities-1977-93.md`) lists 68 Judea and Samaria rows of 1978-1993 as in no note yet. **This note takes 10 of the 68**, all local councils or towns: Ariel (1978), Qarne Shomron (1978), Efrat (1980), Bet Arye-Ofarim (1981), Alfe Menashe (1983), Immanu'el (1983), Giv'at Ze'ev (1983), Betar Illit (1985), Oranit (1985), Har Adar (1986). The other 58 are the regional-council rows of `research_notes/Era 1977-93/binyamin-localities-1978-93.md` (19, #1669), `research_notes/Era 1977-93/shomron-localities-1978-93.md` (20, #1671) and `research_notes/Era 1977-93/gush-etzion-har-hevron-localities-1978-93.md` (19, #1670), see "The 68 rows" below. The Arvot HaYarden and Megilot rows (10) are in `research_notes/Era 1977-93/jordan-valley-localities-1977-93.md` (#1665); the 1967-77 rows are in `research_notes/Era 1957-77/west-bank-localities-1967-77.md` (#1663).
- **Dates.** **None of the 10 founding days is closed from a record of the day.** The documents I could open are CBS (the year), three Official Gazette name lists, one State Comptroller report, eleven JTA bulletins (fourteen more in the second pass of 26 October, J12 to J25) and three UN Special Committee reports ([S], they compile press reports). The State Comptroller report (1999) is the only primary text that gives a date for any step: it gives the founding year of Ariel (1978) and the dates of the local-council orders of Ariel (March 1981), Efrat (1983) and Immanu'el (February 1985). Nine rows carry a disagreement [C] on the year or on what the date marks (a decision, a cornerstone, a first group, a name, a move-in, the council order); Bet Arye-Ofarim is [S] for Bet Arye (all sources read say 1981) with a different step, the first settlers of Ofarim in 1989, inside the same CBS row.
- **Coordinates.** All 10 rows have CBS coordinates (derived to WGS84, method below). For all 10 rows the Official Gazette prints a map grid for the locality or for its temporary name (Har Adar: G3 p. 3396, grid 1622.1364, which is 127 m east and 713 m north of the CBS point, derived). The largest gap is Betar Illit (3.8 km east-west), and I do not explain it.
- **Press.** Eleven JTA (Jewish Telegraphic Agency) bulletins were opened as PDFs and read with their datelines. JTA dates a step for six of the ten rows (a decision, a council, a cornerstone, an anniversary), but no item calls any day the founding day of a locality. **No Maariv, Davar, government decision, Knesset record or Reshumot page was opened** (the National Library of Israel returns HTTP 403 to this server, so every newspaper article that Hebrew Wikipedia cites is unopened). Every founding date is [S] or [C]; the primary items are what CBS, the Gazette and the State Comptroller print. **Second pass (26 Oct).** Fourteen further JTA bulletins (J12 to J25) were opened as PDFs and read with their datelines. They date the following steps, all [S]: Karnei Shomron with settlers on 20 June 1978 and a proposal by 7 August 1977 (dateline; the item says the group was described “here Friday”, 5 August 1977, derived); a Hares settlement "established during the past few days" before 1 February 1978 (not said to be Ariel); Efrat "planned" on 10 February 1980 and "partially completed" on 15 January 1983; Givon, as a town to be, in January 1981; the government's announcement of the site of Beitar on 10 January 1985 and the dedication of Betar on 25 March 1987; the Neve Menachem neighbourhood of Qarne Shomron on 20 April 1992. No founding day of the ten is closed. English Wikipedia's coordinates were added as a second point [S].
- **UN reports.** Three reports of the UN Special Committee to Investigate Israeli Practices (A/34/631 of 1979, A/36/579 of 1981, A/39/591 of 1984) were read from page images. They compile press reports and, in A/39/591 annex VI, a named study, so they are [S]. They add an earlier Efrat decision ("one year ago" in February 1979), planned or held ceremonies for Ariel, Giv'at Ze'ev, Immanu'el and Bet Arye, a Samaria regional council that names Ariel and Qarne Shomron, and a "year of estab." table. The table agrees with the CBS year for Ariel, Qarne Shomron, Efrat, Bet Arye and Immanu'el, and gives 1982 for Giv'at Ze'ev and Alfe Menashe (CBS 1983) and for "Kfira", a previous name of Har Adar (CBS 1986). See "UN Special Committee reports".

## Conventions and tags

- **[V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending]**: what a primary document (a CBS file, an Official Gazette page, a State Comptroller report) prints, read today. It says nothing about whether the printed value is right.
- **[S]** secondary: one source, or several that plainly copy one another. Wikipedia and JTA bulletins are [S] (JTA is press, not a record). Where Wikipedia cites a newspaper, a book or a government publication, I do not count that source as read.
- **[C]** conflicting: sources disagree; every value is given and what each marks, as far as the source says.
- **[GAP]** not closed, with what was tried.
- **Derived** marks a number or a date this note computed; the arithmetic or the library is named. A derived value is never given the verified tag.
- Quotes are copied from the page read. Hebrew Wikipedia quotes are from the wikitext of the revision named in the last table, with link brackets and templates removed; quotes from the Gazette were read from the page image (the PDF's text layer is OCR and misses headings); JTA quotes are from the scanned PDF page images read through OCR (the OCR drops spaces; I restored them), with the dateline read from the scan; the State Comptroller PDF has a text layer whose spacing is broken, so its quotes were re-spaced and the numbers checked on the text; UN quotes are from the page images (the UN text layer misreads digits, for example 1979 as 1919, so no number was taken from it). Translations from Hebrew are mine.
- Status words ("settlement", "city", "local council") are quoted only as a source prints them; the CBS file's type words are given as the file gives them.

## How it was done

- **The list, the CBS year and the coordinates.** CBS, localities file 2024 ("קובץ יישובים 2024"), https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/publications/doclib/2019/ishuvim/bycode2024.xlsx (downloaded and opened 2026-10-25, 370,654 bytes, SHA-256 `a021c6a151863ded624d2f674f9bbcf90ba9f3660c8d55aebe6526ff7f5d2ad0`, the same file as in `research_notes/Era 1977-93/jordan-valley-localities-1977-93.md`; 1,490 locality rows, one sheet; read with `openpyxl`). Filter: column "שם מחוז" (district name) = "אזור יהודה ושומרון", column "שנת ייסוד" (year of founding) from 1978 to 1993, and column "סמל סוג רשות מקומית" (local-authority type code) 1 or 2; a script returned exactly the 10 rows below. **Derived:** with the year filter alone the file has 78 rows; by type code the 78 are 2 (municipality) + 8 (local council) + 68 (regional council, type 3). The CBS intro (https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/publications/doclib/2019/ishuvim/intro.pdf, December 2025, 14 pages, SHA-256 prefix `f45b920f`, opened today) lists the type codes "1 – עירייה, 2 – מועצה מקומית, 3 – מועצה אזורית, 4 – חסר מעמד מוניציפלי" and says in its section on the founding year: “לפי נסיבות המקרה, נרשמה שנת הייסוד של היישוב או שנת אכלוסו בידי יהודים” (as the case may be, the year of founding of the locality or the year it was populated by Jews was recorded). It also says the form of settlement "נקבעה, ככל האפשר, לפי המצב בפועל" and that this "אין בקביעה זו כדי להשליך על מעמדו של יישוב על פי דין" (carries no implication for the legal status of a locality); I use the type codes only as the file's own classification.
- **What the form codes mean.** The file prints a current form code (150, 160, 170, 180, 190 for these rows). The intro says the urban localities (2,000 inhabitants and more) are classified by registered population and that the current form "נקבעת על פי גודל האוכלוסייה ביישוב בסוף כל שנה" (is set by the population at the end of each year). I did not extract the band table; the CBS "Local authorities in Israel 2009" list 03 (appendix C, a key to the localities of regional councils in alphabetical order) lists the localities of regional councils, not only rural ones: its form column has moshav, kibbutz, community and other rural localities and also the classes "2,000-4,999 inhabitants" (35 occurrences in the text layer) and "5,000-9,999 inhabitants" (4 occurrences). The ten symbols (3570, 3640, 3650, 3652, 3660, 3730, 3750, 3760, 3769, 3780) are not in it (0 hits each, searched as numbers in the text layer). **[GAP]** The meaning of each code is not read; the codes are listed only as printed.
- **Coordinates.** The CBS field "קואורדינטות" has twelve digits; as in `research_notes/GDD objectives/settlements-1949-51.md` and the Jordan Valley note I read it as an Israel Transverse Mercator easting and northing in metres (six digits each) and converted it with `pyproj` (EPSG:2039 to EPSG:4326), rounded to five decimals. That reading is mine. No map was opened; each coordinate is one point per locality, not a survey of the founding site, and for towns that grew over several neighbourhoods the point is the CBS locality point.
- **Official Gazette (Yalkut HaPirsumim), primary.** The Government Names Committee's lists. The publisher nevo.co.il serves a captcha page to this server, so I read the Internet Archive copies: G1 = issue 2633, 10 June 1980, https://web.archive.org/web/20131224094153id_/http://www.nevo.co.il/Law_word/law10/yalkut-2633.pdf (704,197 bytes, SHA-256 prefix `e3bcd6b7`); G2 = issue 3165, 24 February 1985, https://web.archive.org/web/20131005000648id_/http://www.nevo.co.il/Law_word/law10/yalkut-3165.pdf (566,094 bytes, `f40db934`); G3 = issue 4012, 4 June 1992, https://web.archive.org/web/20160303170912id_/http://www.nevo.co.il/Law_word/law10/yalkut-4012.pdf (729,916 bytes, `5cfae6ef`) (the same three files, byte sizes and hash prefixes as in the Jordan Valley note). Each issue states the period of the names it lists (G1 to 26 June 1979, G2 26 June 1979 to 22 June 1984, G3 22 June 1984 to 6 February 1992; G3's front page re-read on 25 October 2026; G1 and G2 as printed in the Jordan Valley note's read of the same issues, not re-read here), so a name printed in an issue was fixed within that period; the issue does not say on which day. Each entry gives a grid "נ"צ". **Derived:** the old Israel grid is, to within some hundreds of metres, the ITM less (50,000 m, 500,000 m); I used that rule of thumb (mine, not an official conversion).
- **State Comptroller, primary.** Annual Report 50a (1999), "Local authorities in the Judea and Samaria area" (פעולות ביקורת, רשויות מקומיות באזור יהודה והשומרון), https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/1950-2008/1999-50a/1999-50a-103-Ayosh.pdf (405,155 bytes, SHA-256 prefix `2c91c9ed51d4`, 48 pages; the report page https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Pages/Reports/2821-3.aspx, opened today). It audited the municipality of Ariel, the local councils Efrat and Immanu'el and the Gush Etzion and Megilot regional councils; its page 1 (Ariel), page 11 (Efrat) and page 35 (Immanu'el) state when each council was set up. It was read as text, not as page images. The other seven rows are not in it.
- **UN Special Committee reports, [S].** Report of the Special Committee to Investigate Israeli Practices Affecting the Human Rights of the Population of the Occupied Territories: A/34/631 (13 November 1979, 141 pages), A/36/579 (26 October 1981, 117 pages) and A/39/591 (29 October 1984, 135 pages), downloaded as PDFs from `https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?s=<symbol>&l=en&t=pdf` (SHA-256 prefixes `8b32ffc4a678`, `57439c03fb0d`, `4db58719e30f`) and read as page images. Each paragraph cites the press by title and date (the Jerusalem Post, Ha'aretz, Ma'ariv, Al Quds, Al Fajr and others); I count none of those papers as read. Annex VI of A/39/591 (pages 127 to 135, all nine viewed) is a table "List of settlements established from 1967 to 1983 and projected settlements" taken from a named study, which I did not open.
- **Wikipedia** (Hebrew and English) was fetched through the MediaWiki API as wikitext of the current revision at the fetch; each revision is named in the last table (`oldid`). It is a lead and a cross-check, always [S].
- **JTA Daily News Bulletin.** Searches through the archive's wp-json endpoint, every query and count in "Searches". For every item used I opened the bulletin PDF (`http://pdfs.jta.org/<year>/<date>_<no>.pdf`), read it as scanned page images through PyMuPDF at 250 dpi and RapidOCR, and took the dateline from it. **Dates rule:** the JTA archive page shows the bulletin date; the event date is resolved from the dateline of the item in the PDF and from "today" or "yesterday" read against it.
- All requests used the User-Agent `statecraft-israel-research/1.0 (+https://github.com/eyalgolan/statecraft-israel)`.
