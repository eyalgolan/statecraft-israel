# 1977-93 events research: 20 June 1977 to 5 June 1982

Researched 23 October 2026. Research only, for issue #161: nothing here changes `content/`, no event
is drafted, and there are no proposed tags, options, effects or card text. Every URL was opened on
23 October 2026 unless it is marked as not opened.

**Purpose.** A sourced list of the events of the window the issue sets, 20 June 1977 to 5 June 1982.
For each: the date or dates, a short factual summary, the sources, and both values where sources
disagree. Which rows enter the game, how they are shown, and the sensitivity treatment of the
Coastal Road attack and the Lebanon material are the owner's, and nothing is proposed about them.

**On the exemplar.** `events-1952-56.md` is the format this issue names and its sourcing conventions
are followed. Its proposed sensitivity tags and candidate decisions are **not** copied: the issue
rules them out. The omission is deliberate.

**Scope.** Design §8 assigns B2, B6 to B9 and B24 to #158 (peace with Egypt) and B11 to #159 (the
territories). They are kept here as research for those issues, not as rows #161 writes.

**The Knesset is reachable, and that changes the sourcing.** `main.knesset.gov.il` and
`www.knesset.gov.il` are behind a bot wall and return HTTP 247, but **`fs.knesset.gov.il` serves
the Divrei HaKnesset PDFs with real text layers, and `knesset.gov.il/Odata/ParliamentInfo.svc`
answers**, both checked 2026-10-23. That is the route `events-1952-56.md` already documents. Six
plenum sittings were read for this note, and every vote below is taken from the sitting record
rather than from an encyclopaedia. Each tally was also read on a rendered image of the page,
because the PDFs' text layer reverses word order within a line.

**Conventions** (as in `events-1952-56.md`)
- **[V]** verified: an authoritative primary source, or two independent sources that agree. The
  Divrei HaKnesset sitting record and the Knesset OData service are treated as primary.
- **[S]** single source, a page not opened in full, a day worked out from a JTA bulletin date, or a
  fact resting only on Wikipedia. Wikipedia is a pointer, cited by `oldid` so the revision is
  fixed, but a fact carried only by it is [S].
- **[C]** sources conflict. Every version is given with its source, and none is picked unless the
  primary record settles it.
- **[GAP]** not found.
- **JTA dates.** The date on a JTA archive page is the bulletin date; "today" in an item means its
  dispatch day, usually the day before. A weekday named in a bulletin is resolved against the
  bulletin date and marked **computed here**.
- **No quotes are invented.** Short phrases in quotation marks are copied from the cited page.
- **our reading** marks an interpretation and is not attributed to a source.

## B1. Begin's first government takes office, 20 June 1977

**Primary record.** Knesset OData `KNS_PlenumSession`: 9th Knesset session 3, start
`1977-06-20T16:04`, finish `1977-06-21T00:23`. Divrei HaKnesset,
https://fs.knesset.gov.il//9/Plenum/9_ptm_254065.pdf (checked 2026-10-23), 57 pages, read as text
and on a rendered image. Page 56 carries the confidence vote:

> "הצבעה / בעד ההצעה להביע אמון בממשלה 63 / נגד 53 / ההצעה להביע אמון בממשלה נתקבלה."

**63 for, 53 against.** [V] The Speaker then cited section 15 of Basic Law: The Government,
"הממשלה תיכון משהביעה לה הכנסת אמון", and the ministers signed the declaration of allegiance. [V]

**The coalition figure, not a conflict.** he.wiki `ממשלת ישראל השמונה עשרה`, oldid **43630134**, says
"בקואליציה תמכו 62 חברי כנסת". [S] The sitting record's 63 is the vote; the article's 62 is a count
of the coalition. **Our reading:** these measure different things rather than conflict, but no
source read says so, so both are recorded.

**Portfolios.** Dayan Foreign Affairs, Weizman Defence, Ehrlich Finance. Three were held open for
the DMC, which joined on 24 October 1977. JTA, bulletin 21 June 1977,
https://www.jta.org/archive/begin-starts-tenure-as-premier-with-conciliatory-speech-aimed-at-israels-friends-and-foes:
"three ministries have been left open ... Justice, Welfare and Transportation and Communications",
for "not longer than four or five weeks". [V] with the article.

## B4. Coastal Road attack, 11 March 1978

The owner's sensitivity review governs how this is shown. Every figure below is reproduced exactly
as its source gives it, with no characterisation added.

**Date.** he.wiki, requested as `פיגוע אוטובוס הדמים` and resolving to `פיגוע כביש החוף`, oldid
**44043943**: 11 March 1978, the bus seized "סמוך לשעה 16:30". en.wiki `Coastal road massacre`,
oldid **1375861480**: "on 11 March 1978". [S]

**Summary.** A squad came ashore from rubber boats near Ma'agan Michael, killed the photographer
Gail Rubin, seized a taxi and then an Egged bus, and drove south firing at passing vehicles; the
bus was stopped by police fire north of the Glilot junction and burned. [S] he.wiki and en.wiki,
as above.

**[C] on the toll. Eight figures from five pages, none picked.**

| Source | Killed | Of whom children | Wounded |
|---|---|---|---|
| he.wiki, lead and infobox | 35 | 9 | 71 |
| en.wiki, lead | 38 civilians | 13 | 76 |
| en.wiki, body | 38 civilians | 13 | 71 |
| en.wiki, infobox | 48 incl. 9 attackers | | 76 |
| en.wiki `UNSCR 425`, oldid **1362582952** | 37 | 13 | 76 |
| JTA, bulletin 13 March 1978, headline | 37 | | 82 |
| JTA, same bulletin, Begin at a press conference | 37 | | 76 incl. nine police |
| JTA, bulletin 24 October 1979, on the conviction | 34 civilians | | 73 |

JTA 13 March 1978,
https://www.jta.org/archive/terrorist-carnage-37-israeli-civilians-dead-82-injured-curfew-imposed-on-tel-aviv-area-begin-says:
"Begin said that 37 Israeli civilians died in the terrorist assault and 76 were injured, including
nine policemen, some of them critically. A later report put the number of injured at 82 and

injured",
https://www.jta.org/archive/two-terrorists-convicted-in-1978-coastal-road-rampage. [V] as a report
of the tribunal's charge.

**Our reading:** the spread is consistent with a toll revised over the days after the attack and
again at trial, but no source read states that, so no figure is preferred here.

## B6. Camp David Accords, 17 September 1978, and the Knesset vote

**[C] on the summit's start.** he.wiki `הסכמי קמפ דייוויד`, oldid **43941663**: it began
"ב-4 בספטמבר" and ran "שלושה עשר ימים ושנים עשר לילות". en.wiki `Camp David Accords`, oldid
**1374583077**: "twelve days" in its lead and "from 5 to 17 September 1978" in its body, which is
internally inconsistent. Both given.

**Signing, 17 September 1978**, of two frameworks. [S]

**The Knesset vote, primary record.** OData: 9th Knesset session 146, start `1978-09-27T10:02`,
finish `1978-09-28T03:32`. Divrei HaKnesset,
https://fs.knesset.gov.il//9/Plenum/9_ptm_253754.pdf (checked 2026-10-23), 132 pages. Printed page
4194 carries the full named roll-call and then:

> "היו"ר י. שמיר: חברי הכנסת, תוצאות ההצבעה הן: בעד - 84; נגד - 19; נמנעו - 17. הצעת הסיכום
> נתקבלה."

and "הישיבה ננעלה בשעה 03:32". **84 for, 19 against, 17 abstaining.** [V]

**This settles two things the encyclopaedias get wrong or muddle.**
- he.wiki `מנחם בגין`, oldid **44075310**, gives the same vote as "84 בעד, 13 נגד". The sitting
  record says 19 against. The article is wrong.
- The date. he.wiki `הסכמי קמפ דייוויד` and `הסכם השלום בין ישראל למצרים` date the approval to
  27 September 1978. The sitting opened on 27 September and the vote fell at 03:32, so it was taken
  on **28 September 1978**. The printed header dates the sitting by its opening day, which is
  **our reading** of why the two dates circulate.

**The roll-call is itself worth noting.** It records Speaker Yitzhak Shamir, who announced the
result, as "נמנע"; Sharon, Rabin, Peres and Weizman as "בעד"; Geula Cohen and Moshe Shamir as
"נגד". [V]

JTA, bulletin 29 September 1978, agrees on the tally and adds the faction breakdown and that "The
roll call vote was taken at 4 a.m. local time after a marathon 17-hour debate, the longest in
Knesset history",
https://www.jta.org/archive/momentous-step-toward-mideast-peace-knesset-approves-camp-david-accords-by-vote-of-84-19-and-17-abs.
[V]

## B8. Egypt-Israel peace treaty, 26 March 1979

**The Knesset vote, primary record.** OData: 9th Knesset session 207, start `1979-03-21T10:02`,
finish `1979-03-22T04:10`. Divrei HaKnesset,
https://fs.knesset.gov.il//9/Plenum/9_ptm_254121.pdf (checked 2026-10-23), 174 pages. Page 134

> "הצבעה / בעד הצעת הסיכום שהובאה על־ידי חבר־הכנסת ח. קורפו - 95 / נגד - 18 / נמנעים - 2 /
> לא משתתפים - 3 / הצעת הסיכום ... נתקבלה."

**95 for, 18 against, 2 abstaining, 3 not participating**, sitting closed 04:10. [V] The record also
shows Minister M. Nissim saying "יש טעות בהצבעה" after the result and the Speaker answering "אין
טעות" and closing the sitting. [V]

1979, says "five MKs did not participate",
https://www.jta.org/archive/begin-congratulates-sadat-on-his-peace-move-after-knesset-votes-95-18-to-approve-peace-treaty.

**Signature, 26 March 1979**, on the White House lawn. he.wiki `הסכם השלום בין ישראל למצרים`, oldid

## B17. Osirak raid, 7 June 1981

**[C] on the times.** he.wiki: take-off 16:00, release "בסמוך לשעה 17:30". en.wiki: start "15:55

## B25. The Argov shooting and the days to 5 June 1982

The window ends on 5 June 1982. The ground invasion of 6 June is outside it and nothing here
asserts anything about it.

**The date, 3 June 1982. [V]** IDF and Defense Establishment Archives: "בעקבות ההתנקשות בחייו של
שגריר ישראל בבריטניה שלמה ארגוב, ב-3 ביוני 1982", with en.wiki `Shlomo Argov`, oldid
**1365732096**, agreeing. he.wiki `שלמה ארגוב`, oldid **43994133**, is internally inconsistent: it
narrates the attempt on 3 June but elsewhere says the cabinet met "למחרת ניסיון ההתנקשות, ב-3
ביוני 1982", which would put the attempt on 2 June. Recorded, not followed.
https://archives.mod.gov.il/Exhib/shlomhagalil/Pages/default.aspx (checked 2026-10-23).

**The sequence**, per he.wiki `מלחמת לבנון הראשונה`: the attempt on 3 June 1982 by Abu Nidal's
organisation, which is not part of the PLO; on 4 June Israel bombed nine PLO sites in Lebanon; the
PLO fired about 500 rockets and shells at northern Israel; "ישראלי אחד נהרג ו-11 נפצעו"; and at
midday on 5 June the government ordered the IDF to prepare for a possible ground operation and
reserves began to be called up. [S]
