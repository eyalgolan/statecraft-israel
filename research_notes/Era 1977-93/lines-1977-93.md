## 1. Egypt: the treaty lines (1978-1982)

### 1.1 The instruments

- **Camp David framework (17 Sep 1978), the interim line.** "Between three months and nine months after the signing of the peace treaty, all Israeli forces will withdraw east of a line extending from a point east of El Arish to Ras Muhammad, the exact location of this line to be determined by mutual agreement." UNTS vol. 1136 No. I-17813 (registered by Egypt, 15 May 1979), printed p. 202 (PDF 220). The same instrument is in vol. 1138 No. 17854 (starts p. 53; the quoted text is on p. 55), with a footnote mark in the wording. [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending]
- **Treaty of Peace, signed 26 Mar 1979** (Washington). UNTS vol. 1138, No. I-17855, p. 59 (registered by Israel, 14 Jun 1979), also vol. 1136 No. I-17813. Printed page = PDF page minus 18 in both volumes. [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending]
  - **In force 25 Apr 1979**: "Came into force on 25 April 1979 by the exchange of the instruments of ratification, which took place at Om-Khosheib, Egypt" (vol. 1138 p. 72, footnote 1, PDF 90). [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending]
  - Art. I(2): "Israel will withdraw all its armed forces and civilians from the Sinai behind the international boundary between Egypt and mandated Palestine" (p. 72). [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending]
  - **Art. II, the boundary**: "The permanent boundary between Egypt and Israel is the recognized international boundary between Egypt and the former mandated territory of Palestine, as shown on the map at annex II [footnote mark left out], without prejudice to the issue of the status of the Gaza Strip." (p. 73, PDF 91). [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending] The Mandate line itself is the 1906 line; see `research_notes/Map borders/lines-1906-47.md`.
  - **Annex I art. I** (p. 133, PDF 151): complete withdrawal "not later than three years from the date of exchange of instruments of ratification"; two phases: (a) "the interim withdrawal behind the line from east of El Arish to Ras Muhammed as delineated on Map 2" within nine months (a footnote mark after "Map 2" is left out), (b) "the final withdrawal from the Sinai behind the international boundary not later than three years". [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending]
  - **Annex I appendix art. II** (p. 137, PDF 155), five subphases "within the indicated number of months from the date of the exchange of instruments of ratification": Area I (El Arish, with its airfield) 2 months; Area II (between line M of the 1975 Agreement and line A) 3; Area III (east and south of Area II) 5; Area IV (El Tor-Ras El Kenisa) 7; Area V (the remaining areas west of the interim line, including Santa Katrina and the areas east of the Giddi and Mitla Passes) 9 months, "thereby completing Israeli withdrawal behind the interim withdrawal line". [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending]
  - Appendix art. - **Agreed Minutes, Art. I** (UNTS vol. 1138 No. 17856, p. 162, PDF 180): Egypt's resumption of full sovereignty "shall occur with regard to each area upon Israel's withdrawal from that area". So the sovereignty line moves with each handover, not on one day. [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending]

- **Final zones** (Annex I art. II, pp. 133-134): Zone A west of line A (red line), Zone B between line B (green) and line A, Zone C between line B and "the international boundary and the Gulf of Aqaba", Zone D "bounded by line D (blue line) on the east and the international boundary on the west" (Israeli side, four infantry battalions). ## 2. Taba (1982-1989)

- **25 Apr 1982 agreement.** The award records that the Joint Commission could not agree the precise place of some of "the nearly 100 pillars" before 25 Apr 1982; on that day the Parties agreed to submit the remaining questions "to an agreed procedure which will achieve a final and complete resolution", and, in the interim, each Party "to move behind the lines indicated by the other" (award paras. 2, printed pp. 7-8). [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending] The award's own source for the 1906 line is covered in `lines-1906-47.md`.
- **Compromis, 11 Sep 1986** ("DONE at Giza on the 11th day of September 1986", Appendix A, printed pp. 107ff): arbitration of "the location of fourteen of the boundary pillars" between a point near Rafah and Ras Taba; two other pillars depend on the decision on neighbouring ones (para. 3, p. 8). Tribunal: Lagergren (President), Bellet, Schindler, Lapidoth (nominated by Israel), Sultan (nominated by Egypt) (para. 4). The maps are Appendix C ("MAP No. 1", general area, printed p. 117) and Appendix D ("Southern Portion of the Map included with the Compromis", p. 118); both are page images with no usable text layer, and were not traced or measured here. [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending]

## 3. Lebanon (1978-1985)

### 3.1 March 1978 (context for the 1982 lines)

Security Council resolution 425 (19 Mar 1978), 12 votes to none, 2 abstentions (Czechoslovakia, USSR), one member (China) not participating: calls upon Israel "to cease its military action against Lebanese territorial integrity and withdraw forthwith its forces from all Lebanese territory" and establishes UNIFIL. Resolution 426 of the same day approves the Secretary-General's report S/12611. https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?s=S/RES/425(1978)&l=en&t=pdf and `...S/RES/426(1978)...` [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending] The Litani operation's own dates are in `events-1977-82.md` B5 (not re-read).

### 3.2 June 1982

- **6 Jun 1982, ground entry.** UN S/15194/Add.1 (11 Jun 1982), paras. 12-15: the IDF Chief of Staff told UNIFIL's commander the operation would start at 0900 GMT (1100 local); at about that time Israeli ground forces "moved into Lebanese territory in strength", along three axes: the coastal road in the west, towards Ett Taibe and Akiya Bridge in the centre, and through the Kafer Chouba-Chebaa area in the east. Para. 18: by 7 June the forces, "more than two mechanised divisions", had reached positions north of UNIFIL's area. [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending] (paraphrase of an OCR layer.)
- **Resolutions.** 508 was adopted on the evening of 5 Jun (Add.1 paras. 6-8): cease all military activity "not later than 0600 hours, local time, on Sunday, 6 June 1982". 509 (6 Jun 1982): "Demands that Israel withdraw all its military forces forthwith and unconditionally to the internationally recognized boundaries of Lebanon". [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending]
- **Cease-fire, 11-12 Jun.** S/15194/Add.2 (14 Jun 1982) paras. 2, 5, 7: on 11 Jun Israel and Syria separately announced a cease-fire from noon local time; on 12 Jun a cease-fire was agreed for 2100 local time; "the cease-fire did not hold", with reports of "changes of position, including movements on the ground". ### 3.6 The security zone, 1985

All from UN Secretary-General's UNIFIL reports (OCR text, content paraphrased):

- **S/17093 (11 Apr 1985), paras. 15, 18** (printed pp. 4-5): on 14 Jan 1985 the Israeli Government announced a unilateral redeployment in three phases, presented at the 13th Naqoura meeting on 22 Jan 1985: (1) the western sector, leaving the Sidon area and deploying in the Litani-Nabatiyah region; (2) the eastern sector, to the Hasbaiya area; (3) along the international border, while keeping a zone in southern Lebanon where local forces (the South Lebanon Army) would function with IDF backing. The Sidon-area evacuation was completed on 16 February. [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending]
- **S/17557 (10 Oct 1985), paras. 13-16** (printed pp. 3-4): phase 1 completed 16 Feb 1985 with the evacuation of the Sidon area; phase 2 gradually in March and April: out of the Nabatiyah area on 11 Mar; the Jezzine area and the north-eastern sector, including the Bekaa Valley and the position at Jebel Baruk, on 14 Apr; the Tyre pocket and positions in the western UNIFIL sector on 29 Apr. At the end of phase 2 the forces stood in a strip of land north of the international border from the Mediterranean to the Hasbaiya area, about 2 kilometres deep at its narrowest and about 10 at its widest, to be kept as a "security zone". Phase 3 ran in May and early June; "by 10 June" the Israeli Government announced it complete, while some troops would stay in the zone "for an unspecified period". At that point UNIFIL counted 21 positions in the overlap of its area with the zone: 3 manned by IDF personnel, 16 by the "SLA", and the remainder jointly (21 - 3 - 16 = 2, derived). [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending]
- **Position of Lebanon**, S/17093 para. 38: it would not "accept buffer zones or security zones of any kind" (the Government's letter S/17062, which the report cites, was not opened).
- **Map source.** UNIFIL deployment maps annexed to each report (S/17093 as of April 1985; S/17557 as of October 1985, PDF pp. 10-11). Read as an image: it draws UNIFIL's operational boundaries and positions, not the zone's limits. [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending]
