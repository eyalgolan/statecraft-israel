## 1. Egypt: the treaty lines (1978-1982)

### 1.1 The instruments

- **Camp David framework (17 Sep 1978), the interim line.** "Between three months and nine months after the signing of the peace treaty, all Israeli forces will withdraw east of a line extending from a point east of El Arish to Ras Muhammad, the exact location of this line to be determined by mutual agreement." UNTS vol. 1136 No. I-17813 (registered by Egypt, 15 May 1979), printed p. 202 (PDF 220). The same instrument is in vol. 1138 No. 17854 (starts p. 53; the quoted text is on p. 55), with a footnote mark in the wording. [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending]

  - Art. I(2): "Israel will withdraw all its armed forces and civilians from the Sinai behind the international boundary between Egypt and mandated Palestine" (p. 72). [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending]
  - **Art. II, the boundary**: "The permanent boundary between Egypt and Israel is the recognized international boundary between Egypt and the former mandated territory of Palestine, as shown on the map at annex II [footnote mark left out], without prejudice to the issue of the status of the Gaza Strip." ## 3. Lebanon (1978-1985)

### 3.1 March 1978 (context for the 1982 lines)

Security Council resolution 425 (19 Mar 1978), 12 votes to none, 2 abstentions (Czechoslovakia, USSR), one member (China) not participating: calls upon Israel "to cease its military action against Lebanese territorial integrity and withdraw forthwith its forces from all Lebanese territory" and establishes UNIFIL. Resolution 426 of the same day approves the Secretary-General's report S/12611. https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?s=S/RES/425(1978)&l=en&t=pdf and `...S/RES/426(1978)...` [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending] The Litani operation's own dates are in `events-1977-82.md` B5 (not re-read).

### 3.2 June 1982

- **6 Jun 1982, ground entry.** UN S/15194/Add.1 (11 Jun 1982), paras. 12-15: the IDF Chief of Staff told UNIFIL's commander the operation would start at 0900 GMT (1100 local); at about that time Israeli ground forces "moved into Lebanese territory in strength", along three axes: the coastal road in the west, towards Ett Taibe and Akiya Bridge in the centre, and through the Kafer Chouba-Chebaa area in the east. Para. 18: by 7 June the forces, "more than two mechanised divisions", had reached positions north of UNIFIL's area. [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending] (paraphrase of an OCR layer.)
- **Resolutions.** 508 was adopted on the evening of 5 Jun (Add.1 paras. 6-8): cease all military activity "not later than 0600 hours, local time, on Sunday, 6 June 1982". 509 (6 Jun 1982): "Demands that Israel withdraw all its military forces forthwith and unconditionally to the internationally recognized boundaries of Lebanon". [V, agent read 2026-10-25; a person's check pending]
