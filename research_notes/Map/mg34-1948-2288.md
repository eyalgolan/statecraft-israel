# Who used the MG34 in 1948, how many, and where (#2288, research part)

Status: **draft, needs a person to check**. This is research only. An AI agent opened every source marked "agent read" below on
3 November 2026 and recorded the day it opened each. `docs/CONTENT_POLICY.md` section 2, rule 6, still requires a person to
check every claim against its source before use. This note does not change `content/`, `sim/` or `unity/`, and it does not

## Question and result

#2288 asks which forces used the MG34 between May 1948 and 1949, how many came and when, where a named unit used it, which
`content/map/units_1948.yaml` row that unit has, and whether the "200 MG34" figure that #2277 quotes holds.

**What closed**

- **The 200 figure is not one fact.** Wikipedia (revision 1374483206) says the first contract, 14 January 1948, "included 200 MG 34
  machine guns", and that the *Nora* carried "two hundred machine guns" on 2 April. Morris, as that article quotes him, does not
  name the type for the *Nora*. The Ben-Gurion Archive diary entry of 1 February 1948 lists "200 [heavy] machine [gun]s" next to
  the 4,500 rifles, with the translator's brackets, and no type. A Russian magazine article says the January contract was for
  "20 пулеметов". So "the Czech contract and the Nora brought 200 MG34s" is [C]: the 200 is read by two sources, the type "MG34"
  is named for it by one, and a third gives 20. See the table.
- **Deliveries.** First flight, night of 31 March to 1 April 1948: 40 MG-34 (Wikipedia quoting Morris, p. 117). The *Nora*: 200
  machine guns, type not named there. Krammer (pp. 94, 105-106, page images viewed) names Czech ZB machine guns, not the MG34, for
  the 1,415 and for the totals, and his totals differ from Wikipedia's 5,515 [C].
- **Units.** No source opened for this note places the MG34 with a named unit on a named day. Two leads stay open: a photograph
  caption for Samson's Foxes, and a claim about the 7th Brigade that only a page this box could not open makes (see the table).
- **Arab forces.** Not found. See the search log.

**What did not close, and stays [GAP]:** the 7th Brigade claim (page not opened); the archive and caption of the photograph of
the jeep with two MG 34s; any unit-level count; the Morris pages themselves (the book was not open to this box); any Arab force.

## What this means for #2277's line (facts only)

- "the Czech contract ... brought 200 MG34s": the contract figure is [S] and [C] (200 or 20); the type MG34 for it is on one secondary source.
- "the Nora ... brought 200 MG34s": the sources opened give 200 machine guns, type not named. The 40 MG-34 on the first flight are
  named by one secondary source (Morris as quoted).
- No source opened for this note places an MG34 with a named unit that has a row in `content/map/units_1948.yaml`. The only
  candidate with a row (7th Brigade) is [GAP]. The unit with a photograph caption (Samson's Foxes) has no row.
