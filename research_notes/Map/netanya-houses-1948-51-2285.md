## Question and result

#2285 asks what Netanya's houses looked like in 1945-51 (roofs, storeys, balconies, porches, materials, colour), what share of the
town each type made up, what changed between 1948 and 1951, and how the three models in
`unity/Assets/Art/Settlements/Prototype/Models/` (`generated-1948-01-netanya-tiled-house-original`,
`generated-1948-02-netanya-two-storey-balcony-house`, `generated-1948-03-netanya-flat-roof-porch-house`) compare.

**Result: thin.** No source reachable from this box describes Netanya's houses by roof, storey, balcony, porch, material or colour,
and none gives a share per type. What was found:

- One dated photograph of Netanya's town centre, 1 July 1947 (JNF archive): flat-roofed buildings of three or more storeys with
  horizontal bands and rounded balcony fronts round a roundabout, a sign reading "HOTEL". No pitched roof is visible in it.
- Three JTA items that show what changed: villas in private hands at Nathanya in January 1945; a first stone laid on 17 April 1949 for
  the first of about 900 immigrant housing units in Nathanya and Haifa; about 15,000 immigrants in tents in reception camps near
  Nathanya in December 1949.
- The 1949 NLI photographs filed under "Netanya" that show a pitched red-tile-style roof are titled "an Arab village near Netanya"
  (Hebrew title); they are not evidence for the town (see the table).

Models: see "Model comparison". Short form: 03 agrees with the one town photograph only in its flat roof; 02 agrees in part (flat roof and storeys, with "two-storey" and "balcony" taken from the model's name);
01 (tiled roof) has no town photograph behind it and stays **can't tell**.
