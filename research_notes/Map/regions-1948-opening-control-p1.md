# Region boundaries and capitals, anchored to 1945-48 Mandate units

Web research by an AI agent, 27 Oct 2026. A person still needs to check the history, sources and quotations. This note covers Mandate administrative anchors and proposed capitals. Sources: the Palestine Gazette, Village Statistics 1945, A Survey of Palestine, the William Fuller archive catalogue and the public repository notes listed below. Findings and statuses record the research before the later design approval recorded in the region ledger; they are preserved here without promotion.


Research date: 27 Oct 2026.
Scope: 19 administrative-anchor research items and their proposed region associations.

Binding rulings: regions are a DESIGN value anchored to sourced 1945-48 Mandate geography (Q1). Control on 15 May 1948 is never inferred (Q2). **Nothing below says anything about control.** A Mandate sub-district is an administrative reference, not territory anyone held.

## What this pass can and cannot settle

Each boundary item asks two things. (a) Which Mandate unit is the region anchored to, and where does that unit stop? That is research, and it is answered here. (b) Does the owner approve the membership and the unlisted land? That is an owner decision, so no source can answer it. Each capital item also asks two things: whether the town was a Mandate seat (research), and whether the owner approves it (owner). Because of this, every item gets two statuses:

- **Anchor**: the research part. SOURCED means it is backed by a primary page I read on the page image. PARTIAL means some of it is sourced and some is missing. NEEDS-RESEARCH means it is not sourced.
- **Item**: the whole research list item. It can never be better than PARTIAL while owner approval is outstanding. No item below has owner approval.

- [S2] Explanatory Note, "A" Population ¶3 [V]: "The population considered as 'urban' is (a) that of the four large towns (Jerusalem, Tel Aviv, Haifa, Jaffa); (b) that of the cheflieu of each sub-district, after which the sub-district is named (Gaza, Beersheba, Ramle, Hebron, Ramallah, Tulkarm, Nablus, Jenin, Nazareth, Beisan, Tiberias and Safad); (c) that of certain Municipalities and local councils … (Petah Tiqva, Lydda, Khan Yunis, Rehovot, Majdal, Bethlehem, Rishon-le-Zion, Hadera, Natanya, Beit Jala, Shafa 'Amr, Affula)." Note that Acre does not appear in list (b). That looks like an omission in the original; nothing here depends on it.

Limits on "seat": the Mandate had no act that designated a sub-district "capital". The nearest primary statements are [S3]'s district "headquarters" and [S2]'s "chef-lieu … after which the sub-district is named". A historical capital *designation* by dated act therefore does not exist to be found. The research list's question "which dated administrative act explicitly designates it?" has the answer **none exists for sub-districts**. The district HQs are stated in [S3], a government publication, not an act.

## gdd03-0155: Haifa, Carmel and Sharon (`haifa_sharon`), boundary

- **Proposed anchor:** `[Haifa, Tulkarm]`.
- **Mandate correspondence:** this is **not** a Mandate unit, and it **crosses a district line**. The Haifa Sub-District is the whole Haifa District ([S3] p. 104: "Haifa / Haifa / Haifa 1,021"). Tulkarm Sub-District belongs to the **Samaria District** (HQ Nablus) ([S3] p. 104, p. 145 [T]: "Samaria district, comprising the Tulkarm, Nablus and Jenin sub-districts"). [S3] p. 145 [T] describes the units: "Haifa sub-district is divided by the Carmel range, and includes part of the plain of Sharon and part of the plain of Esdraelon", and "Ramle and Tulkarm sub-districts are principally on the maritime plain but extend into the foothills of the central range". So Tulkarm SD includes hill villages east of the plain. The proposed association has 21 Tulkarm-tagged members, which covers that.
- **Members:** Zikhron Ya'aqov, Hadera, Pardes Hanna (Camp 80 area) and Ijzim are on [S1] p. 128, Haifa SD [T]. Natanya is in Tulkarm SD [T]. **Tag drift:** the proposed association tags Ijzim with both Nazareth and Haifa. [S1] p. 128 has it in **Haifa SD** only [T].
- **Known near-line place:** Ra'anana is listed in **Jaffa Sub-District** ([S1] p. 132 [V]) but plots 0.1 km inside Tulkarm on the repo's lines (geojson `open_questions`). Any boundary vote should keep it in Jaffa SD.
- **Where it stops:** Haifa/Acre and Haifa/Nazareth lines in the north, the Tulkarm/Jaffa line in the south, and the Tulkarm/Nablus and Haifa/Jenin lines in the east.
- **Anchor: SOURCED. Item: PARTIAL.**
