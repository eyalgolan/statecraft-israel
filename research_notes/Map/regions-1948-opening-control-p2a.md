# Opening control on 15 May 1948: Galilee, Valleys, Haifa-Sharon and Tel Aviv–Jaffa

Web research by an AI agent, 27 Oct 2026. A person still needs to check the history, sources and quotations. This note covers opening control in Galilee, Valleys, Haifa-Sharon and Tel Aviv–Jaffa. Sources: the pinned Wikipedia revisions, Morris map key, contemporary accounts and public repository notes identified with each item below. Access limits and uncertainty remain part of the evidence.


Scope: **146 items** on control on 15 May 1948 in the four regions, selected from 742 research items.

## Read first

- **Region key.** The key is `galilee` (`region_name` "Galilee and Hula"); `galilee_hula` is not a region key.
- **Research rule.** No answer is inferred from ethnicity, founding date, geometry, partition or armistice lines, or later events.
- **Status rules.** **SOURCED** means an opened source describes the place's state on 14-16 May, or gives an interval that spans 15 May. **PARTIAL** means the side is supported but no source speaks to 15 May itself: for example only a capture or abandonment date before or after it, area-level text only, or an indirect sign. **NEEDS-RESEARCH** means nothing usable was found. Answers like "Not Yishuv-held" or "Probably Yishuv" under PARTIAL are leads to verify, not findings.
- **Morris.** The Key to Map 2 (pp. xvi-xviii) was read from the Google Books page images. The key gives the **date and cause of Arab abandonment**, not who controlled the place. An abandonment date is never used on its own to name a holder. Morris's body text could not be searched: archive.org is lending-only, Google Books access is CAPTCHA-blocked, and its in-book search was unreliable (no hit for "Tantura").
- **Not used.** Khalidi, *All That Remains*: not online; it appears only through Wikipedia citations, which are flagged. Palestine Remembered: not used (it refused source access, HTTP 403). Sefer HaPalmach and Toldot HaHagana: not online. Hebrew evidence comes from he.wikipedia articles citing contemporary press (Davar, Al HaMishmar, HaTzofe) and the Ministry of Defence/IDF histories. Every Wikipedia quote is from a pinned revision and was compared against the downloaded text (0 misses). A person should still open the underlying books.
- **Repo comparison.** "Repo opening code" is the first character (period `opening`, 15 May-11 June) of the place's hex row in `content/map/control_hexes_1948_49.yaml` on origin/main `0176e49d`. The hex comes from the proposed association row's hex where given, else is computed from the proposed association coordinates with the repository placement rule. For every place with coordinates the computed hex equals the proposed association row hex. Hex codes are coarse (5 km). A hex with a Jewish town keeps that town's code even when a nearby village was Arab-held, so each "contradiction" below is a hex-level finding.
