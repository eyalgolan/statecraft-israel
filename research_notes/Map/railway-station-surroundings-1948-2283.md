## Question and result

#2283 asks, for each station that `content/map/routes_1948.yaml` names, whether in 1948 a citrus packing house, a well or water
point, eucalyptus trees or crated freight stood at or beside it, with a source for each. #2283 allows a first PR for one line
and says the note can grow. This pass covers the Lod to Haifa line: Lod, Rosh HaAyin (Ras al-Ein), Hadera, Binyamina, Atlit
and Rehovot (Rehovot is on the Lod to Rafah line and was read because its tiles were already at hand).

A second pass, on 3 November 2026, reads the same tile series for the stations the routes file names on two more lines: Ramla, Artuf (the Beit Shemesh
station), Battir and Jerusalem on the Jaffa to Jerusalem line, and Kfar Yehoshua, Afula, Beisan and Samakh on the Jezreel Valley line. It adds
the rows from "Ramla, station name" to "Samakh, packing house" below, with positions where a label was read. The Jerusalem line has other halts
the routes file does not name (Na'ana, Hulda, Dir Aban). The stations not read in either pass (Jaffa, Tel Aviv South, Yibna, Isdud,
Dir Snid, Beit Hanoun, Gaza, Deir al-Balah, Khan Yunis, Rafah, Qaqun, Haifa East, Acre, Nahariya, Na'ana, Hulda, Dir Aban and the
rest) stay [GAP] by omission, not by a negative search.

A third pass, on 11 November 2026, reads the same tile series for six stations on the Lydda to Rafah main line south of Rehovot: Yibna,
Isdud, al-Majdal (the routes file's "Ashkelon" point), Dir Snid, Beit Hanoun and Gaza. They were chosen because they are the next
unbroken run of the line after Rehovot (first pass), they run through the coastal citrus belt the `railway_halt` tile is meant to
show, and the same z16 tiles cover them. The rows from "Yibna, station name" to "Gaza, crates" below are this pass. An AI agent opened
every tile on 11 November 2026; a person's check is still pending. Deir al-Balah, Khan Yunis, Rafah and the other stations in the list
above are still not read. As in the earlier passes, a missing label is a [GAP], not a negative.

**Third pass, what closed**

- **Station labels.** Five of the six stations print a station label: "YIBNA STA.", "STA." (Isdud), "EL MAJDAL STA.", "STA" with a short bar, no full stop visible (Dir Snid)
  and "GAZA STA.". Beit Hanoun prints no station label on the tiles read between "KM.168" and "KM.172".
- **Water.** Gaza prints a purple "W.T." about 160 m north of its station label, Isdud a purple "W.T." about 950 m north-north-east (at
  "Jisr Isdud"), Yibna a purple "W.T." about 1 km south-west (by "KM.126"), and Dir Snid a "Pool" about 1.1 km east. Yibna and Isdud also
  print small "w" marks within about 350 m of the station. Only the labels are read; their legend meaning is [GAP].
- **Freight and works labels.** Gaza prints "Custom House" beside its station label. No label reads "packing house" at any of the six.
- **Trees.** Isdud prints a row of tree symbols along the road east of the track, beside the station, and al-Majdal a row along the west
  side of the track just north of its label. The species is not printed, so neither is a eucalyptus row on this evidence.


**What closed**

- **Water.** Two stations print a water installation on the 1:20,000 sheet: Ras al-Ein prints "Pumping Sta." (about 350 to 400 m north-west of the station label at this scale) and "W.T." (about 500 m north-north-west, by "KM.91"), and Rehovot prints small "w" marks on the plots around the station. The printed labels are read; their legend meaning was not opened
  (see "Open gaps").
- **Second pass, water.** Afula prints "P.S." and two "W.T." labels within about 220 m of its "STA." label, Artuf prints "Bir el Hafira"
  and "Sp." / "Ein er Raiyan" within about 560 m, and Samakh prints "Mill" and "W.T." about 800 m to 1 km west of its station label. Only the labels
  are read; what they stand for is [GAP].
- **Second pass, freight and works labels.** Jerusalem prints "Customs", "Govt. Press" and "Power Sta." beside the yard, Beisan prints "Mills"
  about 720 m from its station label, and Samakh prints "Jetty", "Threshing Floor", "Animal Quarantine" and "Slaughterhouse". No label reads
  "packing house" at any of the eight stations.
- **Planted trees.** Atlit and Rehovot print tree symbols in plots beside the station. The sheet does not name the species, so no
  plot is a eucalyptus windbreak on this evidence.
- **Packing house.** No sheet label reads "packing house" at any of the six stations.

**What did not close:** every eucalyptus, every packing house and every crate-freight row stays [GAP]. A map symbol is not a species
and a missing label is not a negative, so no row says "none stood".

## Open gaps

- **Uninterpreted marks.** The "c." printed on fields around every station is not interpreted here; Atlit's plots use conifer-shaped
  symbols and Rehovot's rounded ones, which may mean different things (legend not opened).
- **Positions.** Longitude and latitude (WGS84) are given only for the Atlit and Ras al-Ein marks added after review, read off the stitched z16 tiles to about 25 m, with the sheet's own registration unmeasured. The second-pass rows (Ramla to Samakh) give a longitude and latitude for each label read, to about 30 m by the same method. The first-pass rows for Lod, Hadera, Binyamina and Rehovot still give distances only.
- **Legend.** The printed meaning of "w", "W", "W.T.", "T.F.", "Pumping Sta." and the tree symbols on the 1:20,000 sheets was not
  opened (no legend sheet found in the tile set). Until it is, the water rows are labels only, and no tree symbol is a species.
- **Edition.** "W.T.", "(Stone)" and "29.2" at Ras al-Ein are printed in purple, so they look like an overprint added after the base sheet; which edition carries them is not known. The tile set's edition is not named; three of the sheets also have 1945-52 versions (see the table's opening paragraph). The second pass adds: a purple dashed line labelled "W.T." about 1 km north-north-east of Ramla's station (not beside it, so not a row), and "(Stone)" in purple at Beisan, so those sheets also carry a later overprint. Beisan has a "1949" version, Ramle a "1948" one, and Kfar Yehoshua and Afula "1942" ones, any of which the tiles could show. The rows say so.
- **Packing houses, crates.** No map label, no text. Candidates not yet searched: the Palestine Railways annual reports, the
  Citrus Marketing Board lists of packing houses, and dated photographs.
- **Stations not read.** All stations named in the "Question and result" paragraph as not read in either pass.
- **Tile edges.** Battir's and Kfar Yehoshua's "STA." labels sit at the edge of the tiles read, and Samakh's yard runs off the east edge, so the ground beyond is [GAP]. Ramla's station label was found only on a 5 by 5 block; the 3 by 3 block around the routes-file point shows no "STA.".

  opening paragraph, are read in this pass (with al-Majdal). Beit Hanoun prints no station label on the tiles read, so whether the routes file's Beit Hanoun
  stop was a station or halt in 1948 needs another source (a timetable or the Palestine Railways station list). The thick dark blue line
  overprinted along or across the track at Yibna, Isdud, Dir Snid, Beit Hanoun and Gaza is not explained on the tiles (legend not opened),

  carry a later overprint (see "Edition"). Deir al-Balah, Khan Yunis and Rafah, south of Gaza, are still not read.
