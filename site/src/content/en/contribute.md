Help is very welcome:

- **Programmers:** Unity, C# and Python tools.
- **Historians and history enthusiasts:** sourcing and fact-checking.
- **3D artists.**
- **Game designers and writers.**
- **Translators.**

## Before you start

- Read [docs/dev-setup.md](https://github.com/eyalgolan/statecraft-israel/blob/main/docs/dev-setup.md).
- Run the tests listed in [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/eyalgolan/statecraft-israel/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). `scripts/check.sh` runs everything CI runs.
- Adding a historical event? Read [docs/WRITING_EVENTS.md](https://github.com/eyalgolan/statecraft-israel/blob/main/docs/WRITING_EVENTS.md) and the [content policy](https://github.com/eyalgolan/statecraft-israel/blob/main/docs/CONTENT_POLICY.md). Every fact needs a source, and no quote may be invented.
- Keep game rules in the simulation, not in Unity.
- Working with an AI tool is fine if you say so in the pull request and every historical claim has been checked against a source.

There's no CLA. Code comes in under MIT to the maintainer, Eyal Golan, and goes out under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0; everything else comes in under the repository's licences.
