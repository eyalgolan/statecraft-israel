- A Blender-built 101 Squadron Spitfire LF Mk IXe: a smooth lofted body with the clipped elliptical wing, radiators,
  canopy and a separate four-blade propeller, and the October 1948 markings (roundels on the fuselage sides and both wing
  surfaces, red spinner, diagonal red and white rudder stripes) painted into its colour map instead of floating discs.
  Built by `tools/spitfire_101_model.py`. It streams in a bundle of its own after the first frame, so it adds nothing to
  the web build's first download: the showcase's Spitfire and the 101 Squadron token's Spitfire are drawn with the
  code-built model until it arrives, and keep that model if it cannot load.
