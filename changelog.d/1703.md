### Changed

- Under the gdd-0.3 ruleset only, each city's housing condition now costs and wears (Refs #1703): below 50% condition a
  city pays a housing surcharge of 0.1 Money per housed unit for each point of the gap (about 7.7 Money on the opening
  turn), overflow wears the condition by 5 percentage points times the overflow share, and condition below 25% lowers
  Social cohesion by up to 2 points a turn, replacing the old homes-to-cohesion link. A city's formations no longer count
  as housed there. The gdd-0.1 game is unchanged.
