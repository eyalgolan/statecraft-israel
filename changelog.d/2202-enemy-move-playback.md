- After End turn, the map plays the Arab forces' moves and attacks one at a time and the camera follows them, then
  returns to your view (Refs #2202). The sides play one after another, and a bar at the foot of the map shows the side
  whose moves you are watching, with its 1948 flag (the flag its units fly; the Arab Liberation Army's units fly a plain
  pennant), its name, and each move in words. Skip or Esc ends it; Settings can turn it off. The turn report and the
  decision card wait until it ends. Nothing in the game changes: it shows the turn the game has already played.
