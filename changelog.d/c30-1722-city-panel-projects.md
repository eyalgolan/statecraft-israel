### Added

- GDD 0.3 rules only: a city's panel now lists all of that city's projects, wherever they stand. A project being built shows its work done, its cost, whether it has a builder, and a Cancel button. On the city's own hex, a housing repair under way now shows once, as its row in this list, instead of also in the hex's project lines. A finished project shows the day it was finished and its cost. Under the housing line the panel shows the housing surcharge the city pays a turn as things stand now (or that there is none), how far overflow lowers the housing condition, and the Social cohesion lost each turn while the condition is below 25%. Each value is worked out by the simulation, in English and Hebrew (Refs #1722). The shipped GDD 0.1 game is unchanged.
