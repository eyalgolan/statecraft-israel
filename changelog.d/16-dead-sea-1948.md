### Changed
- The map draws the Dead Sea at its 1948 level, -392 m, as the Survey of Palestine's 1946 sheet prints it, instead of
  today's lower sea: the shore lies further out, the Lisan is a peninsula again and the water runs past it into the
  southern basin, down to about where the 1946 sheet puts the southern shore. Lots and trees stay off the new water.
  Visual only: the hexes and the sim are unchanged. Lake Hula comes later (Refs #16).
