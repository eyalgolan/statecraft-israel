### Added

- Up close, an attacking unit's figures play their fire animation once when a combat resolves: at once for your attack
  order, and at End Turn for the enemy's and the turn's other new attacks. Supporting gun units fire with the
  attackers; the defenders do not, and an attack you ordered earlier in the turn does not play again at End Turn. The
  figures with a fire animation are C06, C07, C08, C09 and C12F; the others stand as before. Designed by Tal Heuberger
  (#2078, #1867), using Tal Heuberger's own fire takes; the models and animations are unchanged.
