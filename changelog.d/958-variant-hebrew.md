### Added
- A reconvergence card's variants can carry Hebrew (`title_he`, `text_he`, #958): in Hebrew, the card, its banner, the
  timeline and the end screen show the variant's Hebrew title and text. On a card whose text is in Hebrew every variant
  needs its text in Hebrew, and a variant with its own title its title, and the Hebrew file lists them under `variants`.
  No shipped card has variants yet.
