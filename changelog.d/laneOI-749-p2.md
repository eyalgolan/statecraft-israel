- The four 1948 operation cards can now give orders to units on the map (R-ENEMY-005). Under GDD 0.1, choosing The Ten
  Days' historical option sends Carmeli toward the Syrian bridgehead at Mishmar HaYarden (the card also orders Yiftach to
  Lydda, but that brigade is not on the map, so nobody receives it), and launching Operation Horev sends Golani to the
  Julis camp. Each order follows the player's own go-to rules. Operation Yoav, Operation Hiram and the alternative
  options give no orders until sources are found.
