- The four 1948 operation cards can now give orders to units on the map (R-ENEMY-005). Under GDD 0.1, choosing The Ten
  Days' historical option sends Carmeli toward the Syrian bridgehead at Mishmar HaYarden (the card also orders Yiftach to
  Lydda, but that brigade is not on the map, so nobody receives it), and launching Operation Horev sends Golani toward the
  Julis camp. Each order follows the player's own go-to rules. Operation Yoav, Operation Hiram and the alternative
  options give no orders until sources are found.
- An operation's order ends with the operation: the unit stops when its anchor's dates in `map/units_1948.yaml` end
  (Carmeli on 19 July 1948, Golani on 24 February 1949, the Egypt armistice). Its march goes round enemy-held places it
  was not sent to, so Golani no longer takes Nazareth on the way. A refused order shows a short message, in English
  and Hebrew.
